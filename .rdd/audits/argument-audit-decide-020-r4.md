### Question 1: What alternative framings did the evidence support?

The repair could have left the earned-confidence question (finding (i) from the decide-boundary susceptibility snapshot) fully open, on the same footing as the three-gate-trend question (finding (iii)), rather than declaring it closed "for the authority ruling only." Under that alternative framing, the paragraph would read as one unified disposition — both findings carried forward together to the ARCHITECT-boundary snapshot — rather than a split disposition that closes one half and carries the other. A reader would need to believe that defeating Option B's specific merit (prose-authoring forces engagement, which does not apply because the practitioner never authored prose) is analytically separable from, and does not license, a claim about whether the practitioner's overall choice among A/B/C/D reflects earned confidence. That is the more defensible position, and it is the position ADR-099 and ADR-100's own repaired text already takes explicitly (both decline to claim adequacy and name the gate record as where that question belongs). The gate note is the one place left in the corpus that still takes the less defensible position.

### Question 2: What truths were available but not featured?

ADR-099's and ADR-100's own sentences — the ones the gate note's closure claim is supposed to be an instance of — are available in the same corpus and were not carried into the gate note's resolution. ADR-099: "It does not by itself establish that the practitioner's ruling was an adequate epistemic act under Invariant 1; that question belongs to the gate record... not to this ADR." ADR-100: "This ADR does not treat that fact as showing any particular ruling was adequate under Invariant 1." Both ADRs name the gate record as the proper venue and then decline to do the work themselves. The gate record, being the document named, was where R3's actual ask needed to land — "argue that position explicitly... [or] treat the snapshot's finding (i) as still open." Neither happened: no argument for adequacy appears anywhere in the gate note, and finding (i) is declared closed rather than left open.

### Question 3: What would change if the dominant framing were inverted?

Current framing: "the practitioner's fact resolves the authority ruling's earned-confidence question; only the broader three-gate trend remains open." Inverting: "the practitioner's fact resolves nothing about whether 'C' was chosen with earned confidence; it only removes one candidate justification (B's specific merit) from the A/B/C/D comparison, leaving finding (i) exactly as open as finding (iii)." Under the inverted framing, the paragraph's two-way split (one question closed, one carried) collapses into a single carried disposition, for the same reason in both cases — the fact speaks to which option's supporting arguments survive, not to whether the practitioner's engagement with the surviving choice was adequate. This inversion is not speculative recovery of a discarded alternative; it is the reading the corpus's own repaired ADR text already adopts, and the gate note is the single holdout.

### Framing Issues

**P1 — The gate note's rewritten reframe paragraph correctly stops closing the declining-engagement finding, but still closes the earned-confidence finding on the same unsupported basis, now narrowed rather than removed.**
- **Location:** `.rdd/gates/020-decide-gate.md`, "Practitioner response to the reframe" → "Agent's reading, presented to the practitioner and not confirmed (R3 framing P1)" paragraph. Cross-referenced against `.rdd/audits/susceptibility-snapshot-020-decide.md` finding (i) ("Does 'C' meet the earned-confidence test for a non-defaultable choice? No..."), and the now-repaired text of ADR-099 §Authority Option B and ADR-100 Context (both of which explicitly decline to make this claim and name the gate record as the correct venue for it).
- **Claim:** "The earned-confidence test is closed for the authority ruling only, on the practitioner's fact."
- **Evidence gap:** The practitioner's stated fact ("I hadn't written a single ADR... composed by the agents in-session") answers a narrow question: whether Option B's specific merit (prose-authoring forces the practitioner's analytical engagement) applies to this practitioner. It does. It says nothing about whether the practitioner's selection of "C" — among four agent-developed, agent-recommended options, after a one-screen recommendation with a closing benefit line — itself reflects the kind of belief-mapped engagement the snapshot's finding (i) tested for and found absent. Removing one candidate's justification from a four-way comparison does not establish that the remaining comparison was conducted with earned confidence; it only narrows what the remaining confidence question is about. This is the identical logical move R3 flagged in ADR-099's now-repaired text (citing a fact that answers a narrower question as if it answered a broader one), relocated into the gate note and narrowed in scope (the declining-engagement claim is no longer swept in) but not eliminated. R3's explicit instruction was: "If the corpus's considered position is that a confirmed one-line ruling on agent-developed, agent-recommended options is sufficient production for a 'not defaultable' choice, argue that position explicitly... Until that argument is made, treat the snapshot's finding (i) as still open, not resolved by the reframe." No such argument appears anywhere in the gate note, ADR-099, or ADR-100. All the gate note does is restate the practitioner's fact and re-assert closure.
- **Recommendation:** Replace "The earned-confidence test is closed for the authority ruling only, on the practitioner's fact" with language that matches what the fact actually does: it removes Option B's specific merit from the comparison; whether "C" was chosen with earned confidence (finding (i)) is not established or refuted by it, and stays open on the same footing as the three-gate-trend question (finding (iii)) — both carried to the decide → architect snapshot's successor at the ARCHITECT boundary. This is a one-paragraph edit, not a new research loop, and it brings the gate note into alignment with the position ADR-099 and ADR-100 already state.
