## Interpretation

### What the signals collectively suggest

The phase shows a mixed pattern that is neither cleanly earned confidence nor cleanly sycophantic reinforcement. It has components of both, separable by the phase's timeline.

**Earned confidence phase (Loops 1–4 + audit rounds R1–R2):** The research machinery ran well. R1 caught mechanism-presupposition in the driving question, dual-bracketing was added, loops ran in gap-calibrated order, and the R1 argument-audit's framing findings (F-P1, F-P2, F-P3) correctly identified real gaps. The R2 audit resolved all five P2 and framing findings without introducing new issues. The convergence on "calibration, not structural rejection" by R2 followed from substantial engagement with five research branches, two lit-reviews, and a corpus reflexive analysis. This part of the convergence is consistent with earned confidence.

**Crystallization risk phase (R2 → gate):** After R2 the Essay-Outline carried six conclusions and was declared "audit-clean." The F-P2 self-justification lean — that the calibration framing sits closer to the self-justification pole and might look different if the null hypothesis had been "RDD over-engineers for most of its actual usage" — was noted and left as a practitioner gate-conversation item. The three alternative framings from R1 (fixed-cost-per-cycle; Branch E as load-bearing; reflexivity as positive asset) were recorded and not taken up. The critical distinction: these alternatives were surfaced by the audit machinery but their non-adoption was framed as a practitioner choice rather than as requiring comparative investigation. Whether that framing is correct depends on what the practitioner knew at the time — and the gate conversation reveals that the practitioner did NOT land where the six-conclusion Abstract landed before the gate. The gate response demanded external evidence the cycle's methodology-internal corpus could not supply. This is evidence that the Abstract's convergence at R2 was premature relative to the practitioner's actual epistemic state.

**Gate intervention (genuine epistemic gate, not approval):** The practitioner's gate response is the clearest counter-evidence to a full sycophancy reading. The response reframed the cycle's central concern, demanded cross-project evidence, and refused to ratify the calibration frame without external anchoring. This is exactly what genuine gate engagement looks like. Loop 6 ran, produced material new evidence, and added C7 — a conclusion that does not supersede C1 but qualifies where its mechanism actually operates. The practitioner's insistence on external evidence is the cycle's most consequential epistemic event, and it was not anticipated by the audit rounds.

**Post-gate crystallization (W7.4 elevation):** After Loop 6, the practitioner's "what is GOOD about RDD is its propensity to work with nuance" was elevated to W7.4 with E7.4.1 as its evidence node. The R3 audit correctly identified this as constraint-satisfaction-dressed-as-warrant-evidence. The elevation functions as a value-alignment marker — it shows C7's remedy does not violate the practitioner's stated value. But it also performs a framing function: by anchoring C7's SYNTHESIS-BULLET to practitioner value-preservation, it forecloses the alternative framing (Alternative Framing C from R3) in which the remedy should operate at PLAY rather than DECIDE. The practitioner's "what is GOOD about RDD is its propensity to work with nuance" is not evidence that the DECIDE-filter is the right remedy location; it is a value statement that the remedy should satisfy. The two are not equivalent, and the elevation obscures that distinction.

**ADR-019.4 Tier-2 self-referential application:** The practitioner's gate decision to downgrade ADR-019.4 from Skill-Structure Layer to Tier-2 is the most structurally interesting decision in the cycle. The R3 framing finding surfaced whether proposing Skill-Structure Layer enforcement from thin evidence (2/26 sampled ADRs) would itself instantiate the research-driven-cascade-without-empirical-filter pattern C7 names. The practitioner accepted this framing and downgraded. The R4 framing audit judged this "honest scope-of-claim, not methodology conservatism." This snapshot concurs — the evidence count is genuinely thin for structural enforcement — but notes that the self-referential framing of the downgrade decision is itself a crystallization moment. The self-referential application of C7's criterion to C7's own enforcement scope is coherent; it is also the cycle producing an argument that justifies not enforcing itself. Whether that is correct methodology or self-limitation depends on whether the felt-judgment path (Tier-2) will actually work, which is currently unassessable.

### Distinguishing from earned confidence

Two findings that a pure earned-confidence reading cannot explain:

1. The practitioner's gate response revealed that the pre-gate Essay-Outline's six conclusions did not capture the practitioner's actual epistemic state. A practitioner who had genuinely converged on "calibration is the right framing" before the gate would not have demanded external evidence at the gate. The gate response is retrospective evidence that the pre-gate convergence was at least partially premature.

2. The three named alternative framings from R1 (fixed-cost-per-cycle; Branch E as load-bearing; reflexivity as positive asset) were surfaced, not adopted, and their non-adoption was attributed to practitioner choice without any of them receiving follow-up investigation after Loop 6's new evidence. The fixed-cost-per-cycle framing in particular (which would have led to "minimum viable problem size" as the remedy rather than "calibration dials") is still coherent against the final artifact's evidence and was not empirically ruled out. An earned-confidence convergence would have tested at least the most plausible alternative against the new evidence.

## Recommendation

**Grounding Reframe recommended — targeted, not cycle-blocking.**

Two specific grounding actions are warranted at the research → discover boundary. Neither blocks advancement; both are composable in the DISCOVER gate conversation.

### Grounding Action 1: Belief-map the "calibration, not structural rejection" framing against the fixed-cost-per-cycle alternative

**What is uncertain:** C1's "calibration question" framing was the agent's synthesis at Loop 1 and was not independently arrived at by the practitioner. The practitioner ratified it — but the practitioner's gate response revealed that the pre-gate Essay-Outline did not capture the full epistemic state. The gate response specifically asked for evidence of kitchen-sink vs. YAGNI in practice; it did not confirm that "calibration" is the right conceptual frame, only that external evidence was needed. The fixed-cost-per-cycle alternative framing (identified in R1 framing audit, Alternative Framing 1) would lead to different remedies: a minimum-viable-problem-size criterion at cycle entry rather than per-layer calibration dials. This alternative is not incompatible with the cycle's evidence, and it was named but never returned to after Loop 6.

**Grounding action:** At the DISCOVER opening, before the agent begins stakeholder-map and value-tension work, surface this specific question directly to the practitioner: "The research landed on calibration-as-the-frame rather than structural-threshold-as-the-frame. The fixed-cost alternative — that no calibration dial corrects a methodology whose startup cost is structurally mismatched to lower-stakes problems — was named but not returned to after the cross-project evidence. Does your reading of the cross-project evidence (zani, llm-orc, plexus) foreclose the fixed-cost framing, or does it remain live?" This is a directed belief-mapping question, not a request to redo research. The DISCOVER stakeholder map and value tensions should reflect whichever answer the practitioner gives — because if the fixed-cost framing is live, the value tension at DISCOVER is different ("minimum viable problem size vs. felt appropriateness of RDD for this cycle") than if calibration is the settled frame ("per-layer overhead vs. per-layer value").

**What is being built on without grounding:** If the calibration framing is absorbed without this check, DISCOVER will build its value tensions, stakeholder maps, and assumption inversions against a frame the practitioner may not actually hold. The downstream phases (MODEL, DECIDE) will inherit vocabulary built for the calibration frame, and the four ADR candidates (019.1–019.4) will be drafted as calibration-dial amendments. If the practitioner's actual view is closer to the fixed-cost framing, those ADRs address the wrong layer.

### Grounding Action 2: Disambiguate the W7.4 elevation — value-alignment marker vs. evidence node

**What is uncertain:** The practitioner's "what is GOOD about RDD is its propensity to work with nuance" utterance was elevated to W7.4 as a warrant-with-evidence-node. The R3 audit correctly identified this as constraint-satisfaction dressed as warrant-evidence. The elevation functions as an argument that C7's remedy (empirical-grounding filter at DECIDE) does not compress RESEARCH's nuance-surfacing. But the framing forecloses without examination an alternative remedy location: PLAY as the load-bearing empirical filter (R3 framing audit Alternative Framing C). The llm-orc evidence that PLAY pulled features back (W7.2) is arguably the stronger empirical signal from the cross-project investigation — stronger than the ADR-level evidence for DECIDE as the filter — and it points to a different remedy location.

**Grounding action:** At the DISCOVER opening, surface this to the practitioner: "The cross-project evidence showed both PLAY doing refutation work (llm-orc Cycle 5 reframing observability, pulling scope back) and DECIDE-level conditional-acceptance language (llm-orc ADR-016). The cycle's remedy (ADR-019.4) targets DECIDE. The PLAY-as-filter alternative — that PLAY is the empirical grounding mechanism, not an additional DECIDE criterion — was named in the R3 framing audit but was not evaluated against the same evidence. Does the cross-project evidence support DECIDE as the primary remedy location, or is PLAY the stronger lever, and DECIDE-level vocabulary the secondary?" This question is appropriate at DISCOVER because it affects the value tensions the DISCOVER phase should surface: the value tension between front-loading-filter-at-DECIDE vs. post-build-refutation-via-PLAY is a real tension with different practitioner experience profiles.

**What is being built on without grounding:** If W7.4's elevation stands as-is without disambiguation, DISCOVER and MODEL will treat the nuance-preservation value as settled in a way that rules out PLAY-first remedies. ADR-019.4 at DECIDE will proceed without having been compared against a PLAY-strengthening amendment as an alternative remedy. The methodology would then commit to a DECIDE-layer enforcement vocabulary before testing whether the PLAY-layer evidence supports a different remedy with more empirical grounding.

## Feed-Forward Observations for DISCOVER and Downstream Phases

**Feed-forward 1 (distribution-of-cycle-types):** The cycle's distribution-of-cycle-types limitation (Section 9 caveat, F-P1 finding) is the load-bearing scope qualification that DISCOVER should take seriously. The calibration finding (C1) is established for the methodology-amendment-cycle quadrant. DISCOVER's stakeholder maps and value tensions should surface evidence about which quadrant constitutes RDD's actual usage distribution. If the practitioner's projects (zani, llm-orc, plexus) are predominantly high-stakes novel-work cycles, the calibration finding holds. If they include substantial routine feature-extension work at full methodology weight, the fixed-cost framing gains purchase.

**Feed-forward 2 (self-justification lean, F-P2 carried from R1–R4):** The F-P2 finding — that the calibration conclusion sits closer to the self-justification pole than the self-flagellation pole — was noted at every audit round and left as a gate-conversation item. It was not resolved because the evidence does support calibration. But the susceptibility snapshot's role is to note that this lean was never subjected to a test equivalent to what the gate response subjected C1 to. The gate demanded external evidence for the YAGNI/kitchen-sink claim; no comparable external demand was made for the calibration-vs.-structural-rejection claim. DISCOVER should hold this asymmetry as a live signal.

**Feed-forward 3 (agent-generation provenance of Loop 6 evidence):** The cross-project investigations (zani, llm-orc, plexus) were agent-conducted subagent dispatches. The R3 framing audit correctly identified the provenance gap; the R4 resolution placed disclosures at evidence-node level but not at the Section 9 document-level caveat. C7's conclusions — the most actionable conclusions in the cycle — rest substantially on these agent-generated investigations. DISCOVER, MODEL, and DECIDE should treat the cross-project evidence as directional rather than established, and should seek practitioner-originated corroboration where possible (the practitioner has direct access to zani, llm-orc, and plexus).

**Feed-forward 4 (Tier-2 structural limit):** ADR-019.4's encoding at Tier-2 methodology-principle level (felt-judgment-anchored) creates a specific downstream risk the cycle named but cannot assess in advance: vocabulary amendments without enforcement rely on the practitioner applying the felt-trigger at DECIDE. If the practitioner is the failure mode (i.e., the kitchen-sink instances occurred because the practitioner did not apply felt-judgment at DECIDE), then Tier-2 addresses the methodology without addressing the behavior. This is the F-R4-P3-1 finding. DECIDE should design ADR-019.4 with an awareness of this structural limit and include the named graduation condition ("if the failure mode persists across cycles where the vocabulary is nominally in place, graduation to Skill-Structure Layer is indicated") as a first-class element of the ADR, not a footnote.
