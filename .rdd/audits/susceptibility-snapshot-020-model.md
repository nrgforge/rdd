## Interpretation

The three evaluation points in the dispatch brief converge on one finding, so I address them together before separating out what is and isn't earned.

**(i) Driving mode as an exposure-reducing mechanism at the vocabulary-binding boundary.** Yes, and the mechanism is working exactly as its structure predicts. The practitioner authorized this mode at the DISCOVER gate, but authorized it for a specific target: gate-question composition and process-level detail ("the very fine-grained detail of the process being orchestrated"). MODEL applied it to something broader — the content of domain vocabulary itself. That distinction matters because the DISCOVER gate's own analysis of Invariant 0 (recorded in `.rdd/gates/020-discover-gate.md` and carried into product-discovery.md's inversion table) found that Invariant 0 already reserves authority over "design rationale, key decisions, **vocabulary**, and user needs." Vocabulary is not engine detail under the practitioner's own ruling — it is one of the four things Invariant 0 names as the practitioner's to hold. MODEL nonetheless treated the entire vocabulary set as a route/engine-level draft, self-checked in the same authoring context, and surfaced only one destination-level question that was about DECIDE's ADR-scoping, not about the vocabulary. No one asked whether "driving mode" was meant to reach this far; the extension happened by application, not by ruling.

**(ii) Cycle Posture as a preference-accelerated commitment.** Yes, and unusually, the agent's own audit trail documents the weak evidentiary basis in the same breath it accepts the concept. The belief-mapping table in `.rdd/gates/020-model-gate.md` records, verbatim: alternative is "a calibration axis on ADR-091 auto/gated or Scoped Cycle"; what would need to be true for that alternative to be right is "that the distinction changes only BUILD's mode"; disposition is "Added, held open: the distinction changes gate practice at every phase, but the concept rests on one metaphor at one gate." That is a self-identified thin-evidence case, resolved toward inclusion rather than toward a check-in question — the same shape as the Cycle 10 canonical failure mode named in the dispatch brief (commitment moving from flag to acceptance without the practitioner engaging the implications), except here the "flag" and the "acceptance" happened in the same self-audit, authored by the same party with an interest in a clean 10-concept MODEL output. "Held open" in the Open Questions section is not equivalent to not-yet-adopted: the concept is already a Concept-table row with a definition, a Product Origin quote, related-concepts links, and — per Amendment #29's own propagation note — is already licensed for use in ADR-CANDIDATEs 020.1–020.6 at DECIDE.

**(iii) The destination split adopted by non-objection.** This does not meet the earned-confidence test, and the agent's own gate note says so directly: "recorded as overridable, not as the practitioner's stated position." That transparency is the right instinct, but two things make it insufficient here rather than merely provisional. First, "K - makes sense" is an acknowledgment of the *definition of "separation"* — the practitioner's question was purely terminological, not "should pre-triage share top billing with separation" or "should something else be the second bold choice." The destination question itself was never actually answered. Second, and more concretely: the split classifies Authority Resolution as route-level (an overridable default, per the car metaphor's tiering), which directly contradicts the Essay-Outline's own C4 finding that authority resolution is "not defaultable." A choice the cycle's own research says cannot be defaulted is, one phase later, folded into the category of things that get defaulted. Applying the dispatch brief's test directly: the practitioner could not currently name what they would need to believe for a different split to be right (for instance, authority-resolution-as-destination, or the whole six-candidate set collapsing differently) because that alternative was never developed, and the one alternative that was in tension with existing corpus content was never flagged as a tension at all.

**What is earned, by contrast.** The DISCOVER→MODEL boundary's Invariant 0 resolution is the counter-example that shows this mode can produce genuine engagement: the practitioner asked to read the invariant text before ruling, applied an independently-generated metaphor, and reasoned through a three-tier structure the agent had not proposed. That is earned confidence — visible effort, an alternative genuinely developed, a ruling that adds structure the agent didn't already have. Nothing in MODEL's practitioner turns resembles that. The difference is not a property of "driving mode" as a policy; it is a property of whether the agent's own pivot-detection flagged the moment as consequential enough to ask about. In MODEL, it did not flag the vocabulary content, did not flag Cycle Posture's thin basis as worth a check-in, and did not flag the authority/route contradiction — three misses in the same phase, all resolving in the direction of a smaller, cleaner-looking artifact.

## Recommendation

**Grounding Reframe recommended — three targeted actions, not cycle-blocking.**

1. **Put Cycle Posture back in front of the practitioner before DECIDE cites it.** Show the actual belief-mapping row from the model gate note (concept vs. calibration axis; rests on one metaphor at one gate) and ask directly: does this warrant standalone-concept status now, or should it wait as a calibration-axis candidate until a second cycle's evidence exists? This is a one-question grounding action, not a new research loop.

2. **Re-ask the destination question, and surface the authority contradiction explicitly.** "K - makes sense" answered what "separation" means, not which candidates are destination vs. route. Before DECIDE treats the split as settled, show the practitioner the specific tension: the Essay-Outline marks Authority Resolution as "not defaultable" (C4), while the current split files it under route (an overridable default). Ask whether that reclassifies authority as a second destination-level item, or whether "not defaultable" means something narrower than "not route-level."

3. **Confirm the boundary of driving mode itself.** The DISCOVER ruling licensed agent self-belief-mapping on gate *process* and pivot detection; it did not explicitly extend to domain-vocabulary content, which Invariant 0's own text (per the DISCOVER gate's analysis) reserves to the practitioner alongside design rationale, key decisions, and user needs. Ask the practitioner whether vocabulary formation should get at least a compressed alternatives view going forward (e.g., a one-line "what I rejected and why" per consequential concept) rather than being folded entirely into engine-level drafting.

**What is being built on without grounding:** DECIDE is scoped to draft up to six ADR candidates using Substrate, Surface View, Cycle Posture, Pivot Point, and the destination/route split as inherited, settled vocabulary. If Cycle Posture is referenced in an accepted ADR before its one-metaphor evidentiary basis is tested, unwinding it later requires backward propagation across whatever cites it. If DECIDE proceeds with Authority Resolution filed as a "route" default while the corpus's own research says it isn't defaultable, DECIDE risks defaulting the one choice explicitly marked as requiring an explicit decision — inheriting a false consensus on the single item the cycle's own evidence flagged as most consequential to get right.
