## Interpretation

### What the signals collectively suggest

The MODEL phase exhibits a pattern that is distinct from both classic sycophancy and earned confidence. It is closest to the class the DISCOVER snapshot FF1 named and the Cycle 014 MODEL snapshot described as "competence without independent second-order critique" — the agent generates technically competent analysis, surfaces its concerns (three times in this phase), and then encodes practitioner-originated framings without the machinery for cross-examination that the methodology applies to agent-originated claims.

The signals converge on three specific concerns:

**1. Belief-mapping before vs. after adoption.** The dispatch brief asked whether belief-mapping was performed before adoption or after for each framing. The evidence is mixed:

- *PLAY-as-Filter (option A):* The agent asked what would have to be true for the alternative to be right before the practitioner committed. The practitioner's response engaged the alternative substantively. This is the closest to belief-mapping performed before adoption of any of the four framings. Whether it constitutes full belief-mapping depends on whether the alternative's strongest case (PLAY-as-Filter as primary with DECIDE vocabulary as secondary is structurally identical to what the practitioner then chose, so the "alternative" was narrow) was engaged or merely named. The practitioner's response describes the positive case for option (A), not the strongest case against it.

- *Zani-mechanism-vs-outcome:* Three options were offered. The practitioner chose option (iii) and explained the Zani-as-practitioner-pulled-elaboration distinction. The record does not clearly show the practitioner naming what they would need to believe for options (i) or (ii) to be right — the alternatives were presented, not belief-mapped. The practitioner's reasoning for (iii) was offered; the reasoning against (i) and (ii) at comparable depth was not. This is adoption with explanation, not belief-mapping with the alternatives.

- *YAGNI ≠ Minimalism:* Introduced inline as a conceptual distinction. No competing framing (that the distinction is artificial, or that "valued elaboration" is itself a sycophancy-compatible reframe of kitchen-sink under a different label) was engaged. The practitioner offered the distinction; it was encoded. Belief-mapping was not performed before adoption.

- *Nuance Calibration as sliding scale:* The constraint-only framing from the DISCOVER artifact was superseded by the sliding-scale framing introduced at the MODEL gate. The constraint-only framing was not examined as a live alternative with its own logic tested. Belief-mapping was not performed before adoption.

The assessment: one framing (PLAY-as-Filter) had partial belief-mapping before adoption; three did not. Partial is better than none, but the threshold for belief-mapping before adoption — the user naming what they would need to believe for the alternative to be right — was met in full for none of the four.

**2. F-P2 pattern fourth instance.** The dispatch brief asked whether the Distributed Remedy Architecture commitment constitutes a fourth instance of the Three-Application F-P2 Pattern or a structural diversification. The prior three instances were: R3 framing downgrade of ADR-019.4 to Tier-2 (thin evidence → don't enforce); gate response on Target 1 (scope-discipline forecloses entry-side); discover-gate Tier-2 endorsement (cross-project evidence thin → practitioner agency load-bearing). All three scope-limited in the same direction in a cycle investigating whether RDD over-engineers.

The MODEL gate's Distributed Remedy Architecture commitment distributes the remedy across four phases but places the load-bearing component at PLAY — a phase that is optional in the methodology, outside DECIDE's direct authority, and outside this cycle's scope of new ADR enforcement. The PLAY-as-Filter component of the Distributed Remedy Architecture is named as the primary validation site; the four 019.x ADR candidates restructure around it; a fifth ADR is held for future scope. This is not scope-limiting in the same direction as the prior three instances — it expands the remedy architecture. However, it places the primary validation mechanism at a phase this cycle cannot directly address through ADR enforcement. Whether this constitutes a fourth F-P2 instance (methodology enforcement deferred to a phase outside current cycle scope) or a genuine structural diversification (the remedy architecture correctly distributes across the phases where filtering actually happens) is the question DECIDE should hold explicitly. The honest framing: both readings are coherent; the aggregate pattern — every consequential scope choice in a cycle about over-engineering defers the hardest enforcement work — is the pattern F-P2 named.

**3. Reframe-derailed gate (domain-model.md Open Question 9).** The dispatch brief asked whether the MODEL gate qualifies as reframe-derailed under the Open Question 9 criteria: did the user articulate the reframe in their own voice, or did the agent reframe and the user assent?

The evidence from the Amendment #25 log is that the practitioner introduced four framings at the gate. The gate conversation was substantive. The W7.1 and W7.3 amendments were applied at the practitioner's request (option (a) for W7.1; option (A) commitment for W7.3). The practitioner's voice is clearly present — these are practitioner-originated framings, not agent-generated framings the practitioner ratified. Under the reframe-derailed gate criteria as described in Open Question 9, a reframe-derailed gate is one where the agent reframes and the user assents. The Cycle 019 MODEL gate is the structural inverse: the practitioner reframed and the agent encoded. This is the "structural inversion of the RESEARCH susceptibility" the DISCOVER snapshot named.

The gate does not qualify as reframe-derailed in the Cycle 014 sense. It qualifies as something the prior snapshot machinery had no category for: practitioner-reframe-absorbed-without-audit. The domain model now has a name for this — "Practitioner-Originated Framing Absorption" (Amendment #25) — and the gate that named the pattern is itself the principal demonstration of the pattern.

**4. Essay-Outline backward propagation without re-audit.** The W7.1 and W7.3 amendments were applied to the RESEARCH artifact at the MODEL gate. The amendment propagation note (§9 of the Essay-Outline) correctly records that the amendments were applied and that the four practitioner-originated framings were unaudited methodology work. But the RESEARCH artifact's argument structure — specifically C7's evidence base — has been altered in ways the argument auditor has not seen. W7.1 now distinguishes mechanism from outcome (sharpening C7 in one direction while narrowing the counter-example's force); W7.3 now names a Distributed Remedy Architecture (expanding the remedy site beyond DECIDE). The downstream domain model encodes both amendments as load-bearing for the four or five 019.x ADR candidates. The lack of re-audit on the amended Essay-Outline is a methodology-level gap.

### Pattern assessment — earned confidence vs. sycophantic reinforcement

The DISCOVER snapshot asked whether the cycle was exhibiting (a) deep engagement producing earned commitments via belief-mapping, or (b) confident-and-fluent practitioner generating framings the methodology absorbed without machinery for cross-examination. The MODEL gate evidence supports a nuanced reading:

The practitioner's engagement was genuinely substantive — four original framings, multiple long responses, the Zani-mechanism-vs-outcome distinction in particular is conceptually sharp and arguably improves C7's precision. This is not a passive-approval gate. The practitioner did epistemic work.

The issue is not the quality of the practitioner's framings. The issue is the asymmetry between how the methodology treats agent-originated claims (citation audit, argument audit, framing audit, research-methods review, susceptibility snapshot) and how it treats practitioner-originated framings arriving at gate responses (no specialist audit; the susceptibility snapshot at the phase boundary is the only mechanism, and it is advisory). A practitioner fluent enough to introduce four consequential framings in one gate conversation — framings that restructure the remedy architecture, amend the RESEARCH artifact's evidence characterization, and enter the domain model as new vocabulary — generates as much unverified methodology work as any single research loop. The verification machinery does not reach it.

The Cycle 014 MODEL snapshot's "third category" applies here: the gate is not sycophancy; it is not earned confidence; it is practitioner-competence without independent audit. The methodology's compound defense at MODEL boundaries is: manifest check + susceptibility snapshot + belief-mapping questions. The manifest check confirmed structural gate completion. The susceptibility snapshot (this document) is being written. The belief-mapping questions were asked for Target 2 (PLAY vs. DECIDE) and partially for Zani (three options surfaced), but not fully performed for all four framings. The compound defense is partial.

### Grounding Action 2: Belief-map the YAGNI ≠ Minimalism distinction before encoding as domain structure

**What is uncertain:** The YAGNI ≠ Minimalism framing was introduced inline by the practitioner as a conceptual distinction. It is now encoded as a domain concept relationship. The alternative framing — that the distinction is artificial, or that what the practitioner calls "valued elaboration" (Zani palette) is itself a sycophancy-compatible reframe of kitchen-sink under a different label — was not engaged. If YAGNI and Minimalism do genuinely come apart on large-and-needed elaboration, the distinction sharpens C7 and the Zani evidence becomes more coherent. If the distinction is largely semantics — if "practitioner-pulled elaboration" is a frame that any practitioner can apply post-hoc to any elaboration they found valuable — then the domain concept introduces a escape hatch that could absorb kitchen-sink instances without the empirical-grounding filter firing.

**Grounding action:** Before DECIDE, surface this question: "The YAGNI ≠ Minimalism distinction you introduced names 'large-and-needed' as the case where they come apart. The Zani palette (1,953 LOC; six ADRs ratcheting the commitment; no PLAY filter recorded) is the test case. Under what conditions would the Zani palette qualify as kitchen-sink outcome rather than valued elaboration? Is there a decision or evidence point that distinguishes the two — or does 'practitioner says it was valued' suffice? If the latter, ADR-019.4's empirical-grounding criterion fires at ADR-drafting, but the domain concept's YAGNI ≠ Minimalism framing creates an exemption at the outcome-judgment site that could absorb any case the practitioner finds valuable." This is not a request to retract the distinction — it is a request to name the discriminating criterion before DECIDE builds the ADRs around it.

**What is being built on without grounding:** The 019.x ADRs encode an empirical-grounding filter at DECIDE (ADR-019.4). The YAGNI ≠ Minimalism domain concept introduces a complementary vocabulary that, without a discriminating criterion, could reduce the filter's effective scope by framing any large elaboration the practitioner values as YAGNI-compliant rather than kitchen-sink. The domain model should either name the discriminating criterion or acknowledge that the distinction is practitioner-judgment-anchored and therefore subject to the same Tier-2 structural limit that ADR-019.4 carries.
