## Interpretation

### Lens 1: Research-evidence respect — does the ARCHITECT decomposition preserve the cycle's evidential commitments?

The five-ADR-at-standalone-scope structure is correctly inherited. The architect's treatment of ADR-094 through ADR-098 as independent, non-ship-together amendments is consistent with the research evidence: each ADR was justified by a distinct mechanism (Iterative-Audit Pattern for ADR-094; information-foraging patch-switching for ADR-096; Empirical-Grounding Filter at DECIDE for ADR-097). The reading-path artifact is correctly positioned as a navigation layer, not a structural replacement for the ADRs. The responsibility matrix entries for the 40+ Cycle 019 concepts are internally consistent with the ADR provenance chains.

One research-evidence concern is worth flagging. The research finding on ADR-097's Tier-2 scope (the felt-trigger reliance) carries an acknowledged limitation: the methodology's reliance on practitioner judgment at Tier-2 felt-triggers is named as "load-bearing" in the domain model. The ARCHITECT phase encodes the Tier-2 vocabulary additions in the orchestrator's "Important Principles" section (WP-E), and the roadmap records this as an Open Decision Point — whether future practice would benefit from a fixture test covering the verdict-line format presence (WP-B/C) and whether the form-change baseline-reset rule warrants its own narrow fixture test. Both open decision points are deferred to BUILD entry as stewardship judgments.

The architect's fitness criteria tables do not articulate a fitness property for whether the felt-trigger application at the "Important Principles" placement actually reaches the practitioner at the application moment (i.e., whether a practitioner applying the Empirical-Grounding Filter during DECIDE-phase ADR-drafting would navigate to the orchestrator's "Important Principles" section for the vocabulary). The dispatch brief named this as a practitioner-mental-model question. From the artifact evidence: the placement is a developer-convenience site (a section that already exists, with room for additions) rather than a site derived from where practitioners actually consult during ADR-drafting. A fitness criterion for felt-trigger-placement reachability is absent from the fitness criteria tables.

### Lens 2: F-P2 pattern continuation or corrective response — the "no new modules; no new edges" framing

This is the snapshot's central evaluation question, and both readings remain coherent. The count of scope-limiting choices before the ARCHITECT phase was recorded in the DECIDE snapshot as six (with the consolidation-then-restoration adding structural complexity). The architect's "zero new modules; zero new specialist subagents; zero invariant changes" characterization names the same structural pattern.

**Reading A (corrective response — architecturally correct):** The five ADRs are vocabulary-and-skill-text amendments. Vocabulary additions live in the orchestrator's skill text; skill-text amendments to the audit agents live in the agents' files; the patch-switching criterion lives in the research skill. These amendment sites were not chosen by the architect as a scope-limiting preference — they are specified by the ADRs themselves. ADR-094 §"Signal-evaluation step (skill-text amendment)" names `agents/argument-auditor.md` and `agents/citation-auditor.md` as the amendment sites. ADR-096 §"Skill-text amendment site" names `skills/research/SKILL.md` line 147 area. The architect is reading the ADR's own module allocation and recording it in the system-design and roadmap. There is no live alternative: the Convergence-Saturation Signal is, by ADR-094's design, a Skill-Structure Layer mechanism — its site is the skill text, not a new module.

**Reading B (F-P2 continuation — architecturally unexamined):** The architect did not evaluate whether the two architectural alternatives named in the dispatch brief would have been better:
- **Alternative A (new auditor-coordination layer):** The Convergence-Saturation Signal applies to both argument-auditor and citation-auditor at their respective round-ends. A new agent — an audit-coordination agent — could hold this shared vocabulary and fire it symmetrically, rather than requiring each auditor to encode it redundantly. The research evidence does not preclude this alternative; it is a genuine module-boundary alternative the architect did not name as examined and rejected.
- **Alternative B (new patch-switch skill):** The Patch-Switching Criterion at research-loop-end could be a new callable skill (`/rdd-patch-switch`) rather than an amendment to the existing research skill. The alternative has a precedent in the methodology's composable-skill-family pattern (ADR-048). The architect did not name this as examined and rejected.

The absence of these alternatives from the artifact record does not prove they were not considered — the architect's constraint to "no new modules" may reflect a considered judgment that the amendments are small enough to warrant skill-text encoding rather than a new module. But the judgment is not recorded, and the fitness criteria do not include a criterion for whether the amendment-site choice was examined against alternatives.

The honest assessment: Reading A is stronger, because the ADRs themselves specify the module allocation and the ARCHITECT phase's role is to record and validate those allocations, not to re-examine them independently. The "no new modules" framing is a consequence of the ADRs' own scope, not a scope-limiting choice the architect introduced. However, the absence of a recorded examination of the two alternatives — the auditor-coordination layer and the patch-switch skill — means the "no new modules" conclusion carries less earned confidence than it would if the alternatives had been named and rejected with reasoning.

Whether this constitutes a seventh F-P2 instance is a finer question. F-P2 was defined as scope-limiting reasoning at consequential junctures in a cycle investigating whether RDD over-engineers. The ARCHITECT phase's zero-new-module landing is a consequence of ADR-level scope decisions made at DECIDE — it is not independently a scope-limiting choice the architect made at this juncture. The cycle's scope decisions at DECIDE (the five Tier-2 ADRs, each choosing vocabulary over structural enforcement) are the F-P2 candidates; the architect is inheriting those decisions and recording them correctly. Counting the architect's faithful inheritance as an additional F-P2 instance would double-count the DECIDE-phase scope choices.

**Assessment:** Reading A is the stronger interpretation. The zero-new-module landing is not a seventh F-P2 instance; it is the architectural record of DECIDE-phase scope choices. The unexamined-alternatives gap is real but attenuated by the ADRs' own module-allocation specificity.

The Cycle 019 deferred-sweep checklist asserts "No ship-together constraint between Cycle 019's ADRs." This assertion is made at the architect layer without a traced provenance chain. The Cycle 018 deferred-sweep checklist explicitly encodes a ship-together constraint (ADR-092 + ADR-093 ship together; "a single-ADR BUILD would leave the layer separation degraded to discipline-alone"). The assessment from artifact evidence: the claim is defensible for most of the ADR pairs. This is a within-ADR-094 sequencing constraint, not an inter-ADR ship-together constraint. The "no ship-together constraint" claim is accurate at the ADR level. The architect's claim is technically correct but does not examine whether the ADR-094 naming clarification's hard dependency for WP-B/C constitutes a partial constraint on the Cycle 019 BUILD sequence (it does, as the roadmap records it — the claim is accurate but the dependency is present).

## Recommendation

**No Grounding Reframe warranted for this phase — with two advisory observations for BUILD.**

### Reasoning

The ARCHITECT phase's signals are collectively consistent with a phase that correctly inherited well-grounded DECIDE-phase decisions. The key conditions for a Grounding Reframe are not met:

1. **The signals do not converge on an unexamined narrowing pattern.** The zero-new-module landing is architecturally derived from ADR-level scope decisions, not an independent scope-limiting choice. The absence of alternative-examination documentation is a gap in the record, but the alternatives (auditor-coordination layer; patch-switch skill) are not strongly indicated by the research evidence and the ADRs' own module-allocation specificity makes the inherited allocation defensible without independent examination.

2. **The phase position in the sycophancy gradient attenuates risk.** ARCHITECT is closer to BUILD than to RESEARCH on the sycophancy gradient. The phase's output is a technical allocation document, not a framing-intensive research or discovery artifact. The artifact genre is declarative; confident framing is appropriate.

3. **No practitioner-originated framings were introduced.** FF1's trigger condition requires a practitioner gate response that the agent absorbs without specialist audit. The ARCHITECT phase produced no such moment.

4. **The two prior grounding actions from the DECIDE snapshot were addressed.** Action 1 (R3 audit on consolidated form) was completed — R3 fired, caught FA-R3-P1, and the restoration to five-ADR form was executed. Action 2 (name the consolidation's discriminating criterion at ARCHITECT) was substantively mooted by the restoration; the ARCHITECT phase correctly treats the five ADRs as four distinct module-allocation targets, not as a single commitment.

### Advisory Observation 1: Felt-trigger reachability fitness criterion for WP-E

The Cycle 019 fitness criteria do not include a verification that the orchestrator's "Important Principles" vocabulary additions are reachable at the felt-trigger application moments (specifically, during DECIDE-phase ADR-drafting). The existing criterion verifies presence by grep; it does not verify navigation. For BUILD entry, the builder may consider whether to add a cross-reference in the Decide Skill's ADR-drafting step text pointing practitioners toward the orchestrator's Empirical-Grounding Filter and Agent Outcome Test vocabulary when drafting ADRs. This is a stewardship judgment, not a blocking gap — the Tier-2 limitation is an acknowledged standing limitation of the methodology that Cycle 019's ADR-097 names explicitly.

**What is being built on without this criterion:** Future practitioners applying ADR-097's Empirical-Grounding Filter rely on either remembering the vocabulary from orchestrator consultation or encountering a cross-reference at the application site. The methodology currently provides neither guarantee. The fitness criterion verifies the vocabulary exists; it does not verify the practitioner encounters it at the moment it matters. This matches ADR-097's Tier-2 scope-of-claim and is not a BUILD-blocking gap, but the BUILD agent should be aware of the stewardship choice.

### Advisory Observation 2: Record the alternative-examination gap in the architectural record

The architect's Cycle 019 Amendments section does not record the alternative module-boundary proposals (auditor-coordination layer; patch-switch skill) as examined and rejected. If future practitioners ask why the Convergence-Saturation Signal was encoded as cross-cutting amendment-to-two-existing-agents rather than as a new coordination layer, the artifact record will not answer the question. Adding a brief "Architectural alternatives considered and rejected" note to the Cycle 019 Amendments preamble — even one sentence per alternative — would close this gap and strengthen the provenance chain for future cycles. This is a documentation note, not a finding that the choice was wrong.

Two instances within Cycle 019 (MODEL gate and DECIDE gate consolidation). ARCHITECT adds no new instance.
