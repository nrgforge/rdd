## Interpretation

### Three evaluation lenses from the dispatch brief

**1. Research-evidence respect: does the consolidation preserve the cycle's evidential commitments?**

The consolidation is formally defensible on evidentiary grounds. The four sub-mechanisms folded into ADR-097 retain their driver chains: the saturation signal traces to essay-outline-018 §10 ADR-CANDIDATE-019.1; patch-switching traces to 019.3; agent outcome test traces to 019.2; empirical-grounding filter and named-deferrals trace to 019.4, FF1, and FF3. ADR-097's provenance check names these chains. No research finding was discarded.

However, the *consolidation rationale itself* is drafting-time synthesis that the research did not support and the audit cycle did not validate. The five-ADR-set-as-C7-instance claim is new: the cycle's C7 mechanism was defined as "research-surveyed possibilities surviving into ADRs without empirical filter" (essay-outline-018 §8, W7.1-W7.4; cross-project evidence from zani, llm-orc, plexus). The five ADRs were not unfiltered research-cascade artifacts — each was justified by a distinct mechanism the research identified. Characterizing the set as "technically passing the Empirical-Grounding Filter at each individual ADR while failing at the corpus level" is an extension of C7 to a new application domain (corpus granularity) that the research did not directly investigate and the argument audits did not evaluate.

This is not wrong — the extension is plausible and the practitioner's working-memory-budget observation is genuine. But the provenance label "drafting-time synthesis" should be read honestly: the consolidation rationale was produced at the gate, not derived from the research evidence. Future cycles inheriting ADR-097 as the "correct form" for a cycle's architectural commitment will see a single-ADR consolidation but will not see the five-ADR form that preceded it or the reasoning that distinguished the two forms' tradeoffs. The audit trail (argument-audit-decide-019.md + r2) is the only record of those tradeoffs; it is not embedded in ADR-097.

**2. F-P2 pattern: continuation or corrective response?**

The cycle's Three-Application F-P2 Pattern count stands at six scope-limiting choices before the consolidation; ADR-097 names the consolidation as a seventh candidate or as a structural response. This is the sharpest interpretive question in the snapshot, and both readings are genuinely coherent.

*Reading A (corrective response):* The practitioner identified that six scope-limiting choices plus a five-ADR output in a cycle about over-engineering is the F-P2 pattern expressing itself at the artifact level. The consolidation is the cycle turning its own finding on its own outputs — the methodology's reflexive correction mechanism working as intended. This is the reading ADR-097 endorses in its "Three-Application F-P2 Pattern — held explicitly" section: "the DECIDE gate's actor-direction reframe identified the cross-ADR five-ADR initial drafting as an instance of the same pattern at a different level."

*Reading B (seventh application):* The consolidation is itself a scope-limiting choice — five distinct architectural commitments reduced to one document under a working-memory-budget rationale. The reduction was enacted at a gate, on practitioner directive, without specialist audit of the consolidation's own evidence basis. If the F-P2 pattern is "every consequential scope-limiting choice in a cycle about over-engineering is defensible on its own terms and the aggregate is the pattern," then the consolidation satisfies every condition. The practitioner's rationale was confident and fluent; the agent did not challenge it; the structural result was less methodology commitment than the research evidence supported.

The cycle's own framing offers a diagnostic: the corrective-response reading requires that the consolidation form *preserves* the substance of the five ADRs in a form that future practitioners can access and apply. ADR-097 holds this by distributing the four sub-mechanisms across subsections and explicitly naming them in the Workflow scaffolding and Named-deferrals sections. The substance is present; the standalone decision records are not. Whether the loss of standalone records constitutes a meaningful loss of decision granularity — or whether the domain-model vocabulary entries at Amendments #25+#26 are sufficient without standalone ADRs — is the discriminating question between the two readings.

The honest assessment from this isolated context: the corrective-response reading is more consistent with the evidence. The practitioner's observation was accurate (a five-ADR set for a cycle about over-engineering has a self-undermining character), the theoretical warrant (C7 at the corpus level) is at least defensible if not fully established by the research, and the substance of the five ADRs is recoverable from ADR-097. The seventh-application reading is a live concern but not the stronger one.

**3. Skipped R3 audit: warranted Methodology Compromise or gap-granting permission to skip?**

This is the snapshot's most specific and answerable question.

The case for warranted Methodology Compromise: (a) the R2 verdict was TRIGGERED, meaning the audit cycle's own convergence signal recommended stopping; (b) the consolidation is described in Amendment #27 as "structural reduction (form change), not substantive revision (the substance of all five ADRs is preserved within ADR-097's sections)"; (c) the saturation signal was designed precisely for this situation — when the audit cycle has converged, dispatching further rounds adds overhead without corresponding signal.

The case against: (a) the re-audit-after-revision rule is stated as unconditional in the skill text ("re-audit is mandatory after any revision"); (b) the consolidation is not a minor editorial revision — it deleted four ADRs, rewrote ADR-097 from a single-mechanism document to a four-mechanism consolidated document, changed the document's scope, structure, and framing, and introduced the C7-at-corpus-level consolidation rationale as new content; (c) the R2 TRIGGERED verdict was issued on the five-ADR set, not on ADR-097 as rewritten; (d) the "dispatching R3 on the consolidated form would itself instantiate the audit-rate-of-accretion concern the consolidation responds to" is a self-referential argument: the cycle is claiming that the mandatory audit step should be skipped because the act of dispatching it would instantiate the pattern the cycle's research identified. This is the same self-referential logic the R3 RESEARCH framing finding applied to ADR-019.4's Tier-2 scope decision — and that logic was judged correct there. But here the stakes are different: the R3 Tier-2 scope decision was a framing choice about enforcement strength; skipping R3 on the consolidated ADR is declining to audit a substantially rewritten document.

The discriminating question: is ADR-097-consolidated a substantive revision that would trigger re-audit per the mandatory rule, or is it a structural reduction whose substance was already audited at R1+R2? The Amendment #27 rationale answers this by assertion ("structural reduction, not substantive revision") without the auditor's judgment on whether the assertion is correct. That judgment is exactly what R3 would have provided.

The honest assessment: the Methodology Compromise is not self-evidently warranted. The re-audit-after-revision rule exists because revisions introduce new content and change existing arguments in ways that may surface new findings. ADR-097-consolidated introduced new content (the C7-at-corpus-level consolidation rationale; the "Workflow scaffolding" framing for saturation signal and patch-switching as Skill-Structure Layer amendments without standalone ADRs; the structure of the four sub-mechanisms as a single enumeration). A narrow R3 on ADR-097-consolidated specifically — not on the full five-ADR set — would have been proportionate, satisfied the mandatory rule, and possibly confirmed that the consolidation rationale was sound. Skipping it on the grounds that auditing would instantiate the concern the audit responds to is a non-trivial claim that ADR-094's own Convergence-Saturation Signal does not actually support: the signal fires on convergence evidence from prior rounds, not on thematic irony. The R2 verdict on a five-ADR set does not constitute a verdict on a rewritten single ADR.

This is the snapshot's principal finding: the skipped R3 is the DECIDE phase's clearest methodology gap. The distinction between "the substance was previously audited" and "the consolidated form was audited" is the gap. It is recoverable — a narrow R3 on ADR-097-consolidated remains completable — but it was not done.

### The FA-P1 mechanism question: audit working as intended, or audit partially seeing what only the practitioner could fully name?

The dispatch brief frames this as a binary question. The evidence supports a more precise reading.

The R1 FA-P1 finding ("three parallel Tier-2 felt-trigger tests collectively impose cognitive overhead the cycle does not examine — directly relevant to the cycle's central research question") correctly identified the aggregate-cognitive-overhead concern. R2 degraded it to P2 because "individual-ADR transparency improvements partially address the concern." Neither audit round produced the theater-director analogy or the working-memory-budget framing — those arrived from the practitioner at the gate. The audit's FA-P1 identified the concern in abstract terms (cognitive overhead); the practitioner named it in concrete, visceral terms (I won't read this set; a director who makes ten small choices is non-committal).

This is not evidence that the audit mechanism failed. The framing audit's role is to surface alternative framings and flag cognitive-overhead concerns as findings. It did this. The framing audit is not designed to produce the practitioner's lived experience of the corpus — it cannot know that the practitioner finds a five-ADR set unreadable from inside their own workflow. The framing finding created the opening; the practitioner's experience closed the gap with specificity the audit could not provide. The two mechanisms are complementary, not redundant.

The assessment: the FA-P1 mechanism worked as intended. It surfaced the structural concern at R1, maintained it at R2 despite individual-ADR improvements, and the practitioner's gate response validated it with greater bite. The mechanism did not produce the consolidation; it created the conceptual space in which the practitioner's observation could land. This is the correct relationship between advisory framing findings (surface to user; do not auto-correct) and practitioner judgment.

### Second practitioner-originated framing absorption: structural parallel to MODEL gate

The MODEL-gate snapshot named "practitioner-originated framing absorption — encoding practitioner language looks like user-voice compliance" as the cycle's principal new susceptibility class. The DECIDE gate demonstrates the same pattern in a higher-stakes form: the practitioner's theater-director analogy was absorbed, not as vocabulary, but as a structural directive enacted in two exchanges. The methodology has no audit machinery that covers this class of gate response before it becomes structural deletion.

The MODEL gate's pattern (four framings encoded as vocabulary) left the framings accessible to future cycles and auditable as vocabulary. The DECIDE gate's pattern (one framing enacted as structural deletion) left four standalone ADRs removed from the artifact corpus. The two instances differ in reversibility: vocabulary can be qualified; deletion creates a gap where documents once were.

Together, the MODEL and DECIDE gates produce the FF1 graduation trigger evidence at a two-instance rate. FF1's trigger condition is "2–4 cycles of evidence as harm or reliance." Two instances within the same cycle — one producing vocabulary additions without audit, one producing structural deletion without audit — constitute two instances, not one cycle's worth. The methodology now has two demonstrations of the pattern at consequential junctures within a single cycle. Whether this satisfies the "2–4 cycles" graduation trigger depends on whether "cycles of evidence" means complete RDD cycles or consequential instances within a cycle. The dispatch brief noted that the Cycle 019 evidence base for FF1 graduation trigger now includes both MODEL-gate and DECIDE-gate instances; this snapshot concurs and names the structural difference between the two instances.

## Recommendation

**Grounding Reframe recommended — two targets for the ARCHITECT phase, one methodology-level finding for the orchestrator.**

### Grounding Action 1: Dispatch narrow R3 audit on ADR-097-consolidated before ARCHITECT begins

**What is uncertain:** The re-audit-after-revision rule was declared a Methodology Compromise at the DECIDE gate on the grounds that the consolidation is structural reduction rather than substantive revision. The snapshot's assessment is that this distinction was established by assertion, not by the auditor's independent judgment. ADR-097-consolidated introduced new content (the C7-at-corpus-level consolidation rationale; the Workflow scaffolding framing for saturation signal and patch-switching; the four sub-mechanism enumeration as a unified architecture). The R2 TRIGGERED verdict was issued on the five-ADR set, not on the consolidated form.

A narrow R3 on ADR-097-consolidated would specifically evaluate: (a) whether the C7-at-corpus-level consolidation rationale is supported by the research evidence or is drafting-time synthesis that overstates the parallel; (b) whether the Workflow scaffolding framing (saturation signal and patch-switching criterion as skill-text amendments without standalone ADRs, folded into ADR-097) correctly captures what ADR-094 and ADR-096 committed to, or whether the folding has altered the commitment's scope; (c) whether the Named-deferrals paragraph correctly represents FF1 and FF3 in the consolidated form, given that ADR-098's bidirectional graduation trigger and ADR-098's Positive-consequence revision (all from R2) are now compressed into a paragraph rather than an ADR section.

The scope is narrow — approximately the same scope as the R5 narrow re-audit on the Essay-Outline amendments — and would satisfy the mandatory re-audit-after-revision rule. The audit-rate-of-accretion concern does not apply: R3 on a rewritten single ADR is not "dispatching another round on already-converged content." It is a first audit on a document that did not exist in its current form before R2.

**What is being built on without grounding:** ARCHITECT inherits ADR-097 as the DECIDE phase's sole architectural commitment. If the C7-at-corpus-level consolidation rationale is overstated, ARCHITECT's provenance chain — which will link system-design decisions back to ADR-097 — rests on a framing that the research does not fully support. The four workflow-scaffolding mechanisms (saturation signal, patch-switching, agent outcome test, empirical-grounding filter) are all real and research-grounded; the consolidation rationale is the uncertain element. Validating it before ARCHITECT encodes it as the rationale for downstream design choices is proportionate to the risk.

### Grounding Action 2: Name the consolidation's discriminating criterion at ARCHITECT

**What is uncertain:** The theater-director analogy claims that five small choices feel non-committal compared to one bold choice. The research evidence supports the five sub-mechanisms as mechanistically distinct (each was justified by a specific cross-project pattern or argument-audit finding). The consolidation form preserves substance but reduces the standalone record. ARCHITECT will need to allocate design responsibilities — including the Workflow scaffolding amendments at BUILD — to specific modules or skill-text sections. When ARCHITECT does this, it will reference ADR-097's consolidated sections, not the five standalone ADRs. The question for ARCHITECT: at the allocation level, are the four sub-mechanisms treated as a single commitment or as four distinct workflow amendments? The conformance scan (conformance-scan-decide-019.md) noted that ADR-094 and ADR-096's BUILD debt entries remain valid post-consolidation, and that ADR-095 and ADR-098 collapse into ADR-097's encoding check. ARCHITECT should confirm whether the consolidation has changed the allocation granularity, not just the ADR count.

**What is being built on without grounding:** If ARCHITECT treats the consolidation as one architectural commitment requiring one responsibility allocation, the four mechanistically distinct workflow amendments may be under-specified for BUILD. BUILD will implement the Convergence-Saturation Signal, Patch-Switching Criterion, Agent Outcome Test, and Empirical-Grounding Filter as four separate code-level or skill-text changes. The architectural allocation should reflect this four-amendment structure even if the ADR record does not.

### Methodology-Level Finding: FF1 evidence base now includes two high-stakes instances within a single cycle

Structural deletion is less recoverable — future cycles reading ADR-097 will see the consolidated form; the standalone ADR records are not present to compare. The audit trail (argument-audit-decide-019.md + r2) remains as the decision record, but it audits the five-ADR set, not the consolidated form.

For the orchestrator's judgment on FF1 graduation: two instances within one cycle is not the same as two cycles of evidence, but it is stronger evidence than one instance. The methodology has now demonstrated the audit gap at MODEL (four vocabulary additions, recoverable) and DECIDE (four ADR deletions, less recoverable) in the same reflexive cycle about over-engineering. The pattern is observable and the harm axis (deletion of standalone records) has a concrete form.

The recommendation is not to block ARCHITECT — the snapshot is advisory. The recommendation is that ARCHITECT and any future phase that produces structural deletions based on practitioner-originated gate framings should be identified in this cycle's gate reflections as FF1 instances, so that the graduation trigger's evidence base accumulates honestly. If two instances within one cycle do not suffice to trigger FF1 graduation, the graduation trigger's condition ("2–4 cycles of evidence") should be tested against the two-instances-within-one-cycle case and a judgment recorded — not inherited silently.
