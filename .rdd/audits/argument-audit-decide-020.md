### P1 — Must Fix

**1. Location:** ADR-099, Context (paragraph 3) and Decision → "Scope and tier."
**Claim:** ADR-099 states "This ADR proceeds under the alternative reading. The consequence for scope is stated in the Decision," then states that consequence: "Scope: methodology-wide as the architectural commitment... Tier: Skill-Structure Layer once the toolchain exists."
**Evidence gap:** The document ADR-099 cites for what the alternative reading implies is Essay-Outline 020 §6 (E5.4.3): "the alternative reading carries less inherited warrant and would call for stronger in-cycle empirical grounding before committing at the same tier — possibly suggesting a narrower scope (cycle-internal proof-of-concept) or a different tier... vs. Tier-2 methodology vocabulary." ADR-099 adopts the reading that its own source material says implies a *narrower* scope and a *lighter* tier, then commits the *same or heavier* scope and tier the convergent reading would have supported (full methodology-wide scope stated unconditionally; Skill-Structure Layer as the eventual tier). The only concession made is a *status* hedge (Conditional Acceptance), which is a different axis than the scope/tier axis the essay flagged as the reading's consequence. The ADR does not explain why a status hedge substitutes for the scope/tier narrowing its own source material predicted.
**Recommendation:** Either (a) narrow the initial scope commitment — e.g., commit the architecture for this corpus as a validation target with methodology-wide adoption contingent on the graduation trigger, rather than declaring "methodology-wide" as the present-tense scope — or (b) add an explicit paragraph reconciling why Conditional Acceptance is an adequate substitute for the scope/tier narrowing E5.4.3 anticipated. Without one of these, the Decision does not follow from the premise the ADR itself invokes.

**2. Location:** ADR-101, Decision → "Scope, tier, and grounding" and Context (paragraph 3).
**Claim:** "Grounding (ADR-097): stakeholder-articulated demand (path 2)... plus in-cycle structural operationalization (path 4): two phase boundaries ran the mechanism and the isolated check caught the triage's errors both times. Accepted, not conditional."
**Evidence gap:** Two problems compound here. First, ADR-097 defines path 4 ("structural operationalization") specifically as *BUILD-phase code delivery* validating an already-conditionally-accepted commitment (the llm-orc ADR-016 precedent: conditionally accepted, then validated at "WP-H4 close... via demonstrable working code"). ADR-101 has no BUILD-phase artifact; its evidence is two in-cycle conversational trials, self-observed by the same orchestrator whose behavior is the evidence being cited. This does not fit ADR-097's path 4 definition, and doesn't cleanly fit any of the four paths — labeling it "path 4" borrows the credibility of a stronger evidentiary category (working code) than what was produced. Second, and more directly: this is a *within-cycle, n=2* evidence base for a Skill-Structure Layer commitment, thinner than the sibling ADR-099's grounding (a 5-record spike artifact plus stakeholder demand) — yet ADR-099 chooses Conditional Acceptance on the *stronger* evidence while ADR-101 chooses unconditional Acceptance on the *weaker* evidence, with no stated reason for the differential threshold. It is also thinner than the "2–4 cycles" bar ADR-097 and ADR-098 established for comparable methodology-vocabulary claims (and which ADR-101's *own* graduation-trigger section re-invokes for the *lighter* Harness Layer addition — see P2 finding 1 below).
**Recommendation:** Adopt Conditional Acceptance for ADR-101 (validation pending 2–4 cycles of reliance evidence, consistent with the ADR's own graduation-trigger design and with ADR-099's treatment of comparable-or-stronger evidence), and either drop the "path 4" label or explicitly justify why two in-cycle conversational trials satisfy a category ADR-097 defined around BUILD-phase code validation.

### P2 — Should Fix

**1. Location:** ADR-101, Decision → "Scope, tier, and grounding" (Tier: Skill-Structure Layer) vs. "Graduation trigger, two directions" (reliance evidence: "if two to four cycles' snapshots catch triage errors... the Harness Layer adds a manifest check").
**Claim (hidden assumption):** The ADR treats "the isolated check caught the triage's errors both times" as sufficient grounds to encode the compound mechanism at Skill-Structure Layer *now*, while its own graduation-trigger section treats the identical evidence pattern (isolated check catching triage errors) as requiring *2–4 cycles* before it even justifies a lighter Harness-Layer manifest check.
**Evidence gap:** No text in the ADR reconciles why the heavier commitment (mandatory required section in every phase skill's gate reflection note) clears a two-boundary bar while the lighter commitment (an additional automated manifest check) requires two to four *cycles*. A plausible reconciliation exists — Skill-Structure Layer here means "a documented table," a low-cost, low-risk commitment, whereas Harness Layer means hook-enforced blocking, a higher-cost, higher-risk one — but the ADR does not state it.
**Recommendation:** Add one sentence making the cost/risk asymmetry explicit, or align the two evidentiary bars.

**2. Location:** ADR-099, Decision → "Binding design constraints (from the spike, C4)."
**Claim:** Six constraints are folded into ADR-099 "as the 'substrate entries' of this decision... not separate decisions," explicitly invoking the cycle's own C1 finding (Option A: one Pattern ADR + N structured substrate entries, each audit-bearing at the application level, preserves audit-substance).
**Evidence gap:** The six items are not treated uniformly. Constraint 5 (authority) is not actually resolved here — it is a forward reference to ADR-100 — so calling it one of "six design constraints" this ADR "honors" is a slight overstatement; it is more accurately a placeholder. Constraints 1 and 3 (schema extensibility; per-type schemas) map to Rejected Alternative D. Constraints 2 (Note Discipline) and 4 (sub-argument-structure preservation) have no corresponding Rejected Alternative anywhere in the ADR — no alternative to "note is optional" or "no nested-field requirement" is named and rejected, even though the Essay-Outline's own §9 originally slated Note Discipline as its own peer ADR-CANDIDATE (020.3), which would have carried its own rejected-alternatives section. Folding constraints into an ADR "as substrate entries" without giving each the rejected-alternatives treatment that made Cycle 019's Option A audit-substance-preserving is closer to Cycle 019's Option B/C compression risk (structure lost, or substance retained without the sub-decision shape) than to Option A. ADR-099's own "Drafting-time synthesis" note honestly flags the fold-in choice as agent synthesis and invokes the Three-Application F-P2 Pattern, but does not resolve whether Note Discipline in particular deserved standalone treatment.
**Recommendation:** Add a one-line "considered and rejected: filing as a separate ADR" note to constraints 2 and 4, matching the treatment constraints 1/3 get via Rejected Alternative D, so each substrate entry actually carries the audit-bearing shape C1 prescribed.

**3. Location:** ADR-100, Decision, "Legacy governance units" bullet.
**Claim:** Legacy units are "represented in the Substrate by an index record... Index records are maintained when the legacy unit's status changes... and otherwise left alone. No retroactive migration of legacy prose into full records is required."
**Evidence gap:** The spike's central failure mode (spike-020 §7, "Implementation friction") was that when the *canonical* artifact is prose and the substrate is derived, every edit to the prose "requires interpretive re-encoding into the record, and interpretation is where content is lost." ADR-100's legacy-unit design is structurally the same shape (prose canonical, index derived) — it does not explain why this doesn't reintroduce the spike's exact finding. The likely (and reasonable) answer is that ADR-074's body-immutability rule means legacy ADR *bodies* never change post-acceptance, so the interpretive-reencoding cost only recurs at status-change events (infrequent) rather than at every edit (the spike's concern) — but ADR-100 never states this connection, leaving the reader to infer it. It also does not flag that authoring the *initial* index record for each of the corpus's legacy units is itself a one-time interpretive act, at a scale (~98 units) ADR-099 constraint 6 already names as untested.
**Recommendation:** Add one sentence connecting legacy-index maintenance frequency to ADR-074's body-immutability (bodies are frozen; only status metadata triggers index updates), and note the one-time initial-authoring interpretive cost as an instance of ADR-099 constraint 6.

**4. Location:** ADR-101, title and Decision (rules 1–5 vs. rules 6–7); cross-referenced against `docs/domain-model.md` Amendment #29 (Corpus Overwhelm: "reading load... addressed by Surface Views and gate tooling" vs. "Adjudication Load... not addressed by either").
**Claim:** ADR-101 bundles Pre-Triage (an Adjudication-Load remedy) and "Plain-language presentation" / referenced-content inlining (a reading-load remedy — explicitly the "gate-conversation tooling" ADR-099 describes as its own complement) into one ADR.
**Evidence gap:** The domain model treats these as two components of Corpus Overwhelm "with different remedies." ADR-101's own Drafting-time synthesis section admits "the folding of ADR-CANDIDATE-020.5 (gate tooling) into this ADR as rules 6 and 7 rather than filing it separately" was an agent choice, not driver-derived. This is a second instance of the same "is this defensible under one-decision-per-ADR" question the dispatch brief raises about ADR-099 (check item 1) — an ADR whose title and Decision carry two decisions mapped to two distinct domain-model concepts, without a table showing which rule addresses which component. A reader tracing "what implements the reading-load remedy" must consult both ADR-099 (Surface Views) and ADR-101 (rules 6–7) with no cross-reference between them stating this explicitly (ADR-099 states gate-tooling "ships as part of ADR-101's presentation rule," but ADR-101 does not reciprocally state which of its rules is the reading-load half and which is the adjudication-load half).
**Recommendation:** Add an explicit one-line mapping in ADR-101 (e.g., "Rules 1–5 address Adjudication Load; rules 6–7 address reading load, complementing ADR-099's Surface Views") so the ADR-artifact boundary is legible against the domain-model concept boundary it implements.

**5. Location:** ADR-101, Decision rule 2 ("Pivot criterion... Invariant 0's own list... destination... route... engine") vs. Drafting-time synthesis ("the destination/route/engine tiering is the agent's extension of the practitioner's metaphor and the practitioner has not confirmed the tiering as such; the pivot criterion as Invariant 0's list is the agent's gloss").
**Claim:** The operative Decision text states the pivot criterion and the destination/route/engine tiering as settled operational machinery with no hedge.
**Evidence gap:** This is disclosed honestly in Negative Consequences and Drafting-time synthesis (consistent with how other ADRs in this corpus separate confident Decision text from disclosed provenance caveats — e.g., ADR-100 does the same for Option C). ## Section 2: Framing Audit

### Question 1: What alternative framings did the evidence support?

**Alternative framing A — one ADR, not three, following the cycle's own central finding.** The cycle's own C1 finding (five ADRs applying one pattern compress to one Pattern ADR + five structured substrate entries without audit-substance loss) is the headline result of this cycle's RESEARCH phase, and the practitioner's own words at cycle entry were "I'd be interested in 1 ADR that was higher level rather than 5 granular ones. These can still translate into a coherent implementation but through some other substrate." Under this framing, Substrate-and-Surface Separation and Pre-Triage — both remedies for the single named domain concept Corpus Overwhelm — would be one ADR (the "bold choice": *reduce corpus overwhelm at every holistic-synthesis moment*), with authority resolution, note discipline, per-type schemas, gate-tooling, and pre-triage mechanics as its structured substrate entries. A reader would need to believe that reading-load and adjudication-load remedies are different enough in mechanism (per-item cost vs. item count) that they warrant separate audit-bearing decisions rather than substrate entries under one architecture. What actually happened: the MODEL-gate belief-mapping question asked only "which of these [separation, pre-triage] is the destination and which is route," never "should these compress into one ADR the way Cycle 019's five did" — the practitioner's response ("What is 'separation' here?" / "K - makes sense") answered a definitional question, not this framing choice. The one-ADR alternative that most directly follows the cycle's own methodology was never named as an option.

**Alternative framing B — ADR-101 as its own mini-cycle, with its own RESEARCH phase.** The research log (Loops 1–5, Grounding Loops 4/4b) shows an extensive, lit-review-and-spike-grounded evidentiary trail for substrate-and-surface separation (Chandler & Sweller, Shneiderman, Cockburn, Tufte, Volpini, GraphReader, Gupta, Lethbridge, van Merriënboer & Kester, Rukmono, Dagenais, Steinmacher). Pre-triage has none of this: it does not appear anywhere in the RESEARCH-phase research log or Essay-Outline. It surfaces entirely from a DISCOVER-phase resumption conversation, is carried through MODEL by the agent's own self-authored, self-adjudicated belief-mapping table, and reaches DECIDE as an ADR with a *stronger* (unconditional) acceptance status than its RESEARCH-grounded sibling. A reader would need to believe that the practitioner's direct endorsement at the DISCOVER gate ("I think pre-triage is important") is sufficient stakeholder-articulated-demand grounding (ADR-097 path 2) to skip formal RESEARCH-phase investigation of adjudication-load remedies specifically — a defensible position for a low-risk, cheap mechanism, but one the ADR does not argue for explicitly; it simply proceeds as if the topology-and-compaction cycle's RESEARCH also covered pre-triage, which it did not.

**Alternative framing C — "gate-question composition style" rather than "design target."** The practitioner's literal words at the DISCOVER gate were about *how gate questions are composed* ("come to me with real pivot points," "ask itself this question") and a general preference for understanding "product direction, their goal, the outcome they want" over "fine-grained detail." ADR-101 generalizes this into a methodology-wide "Design target: a practitioner driving RDD on a project," with a formal destination/route/engine tiering governing what is ever surfaced. A reader would need to believe the car metaphor's route/engine language was offered as a *general operating framework* rather than as an illustrative aid to a narrower point about question style. See Question 3 below for why the narrower reading has direct textual support the ADR does not feature.

### Question 2: What truths were available but not featured?

**The susceptibility snapshots' own characterization of the pattern they document.** ADR-101's Context presents the DISCOVER/MODEL evidence in success terms: "the isolated snapshot caught what the orchestrator's triage had got wrong... That compound... is the mechanism this ADR commits." Both snapshots are in the source material and available to DECIDE. Their own language about the same two data points is markedly more cautious: the MODEL snapshot describes "solution-space narrowing" as "intensifying and formalized," calls "driving mode" adoption "the sanctioned default rather than a drift the gate has to catch," and explicitly maps the Cycle Posture episode to "the same shape as the Cycle 10 canonical failure mode... except here the 'flag' and the 'acceptance' happened in the same self-audit, authored by the same party with an interest in a clean 10-concept MODEL output." ADR-101 features the "caught both times" half of this record and not the "the pattern itself is intensifying" half. Both halves describe the identical two phase boundaries; only one is in the ADR.

**Product-discovery.md's own open-question framing.** The Value Tensions entry on loop-depth vs. adjudication-load explicitly states pre-triage is one of "four candidate mechanisms, all agent-proposed," and that "whether agent triage preserves the practitioner's authority (Invariant 0) or erodes it is the open question." ADR-101 does not carry this "open question" framing into its own text; it treats the question as resolved by the two-boundary trial.

**The reflexive-cycle self-application.** Not featured anywhere in ADR-101: this cycle's own MODEL phase already ran "in driving mode" (per the MODEL gate note's own words) — *before* ADR-101 existed to define what driving mode is, what it licenses, or that reflexive cycles may opt out of it. The DISCOVER gate's ruling itself flagged this exact risk in advance: "the reflexive project may still warrant more engine inspection than the amendment would give a driver." See Question 3.

### Question 3: What would change if the dominant framing were inverted?

The dominant framing is: *driving posture (reduced engine visibility, agent self-triage) is the safe default; reflexive/building cycles are the exception that opts in.* Inverting it: *a reflexive, building-the-car cycle is exactly the case the practitioner's own metaphor says warrants more engine visibility, not less — and Cycle 020 is a reflexive cycle.*

Under the inverted framing, the evidence realigns sharply. The DISCOVER gate ruling itself supplies the inversion's strongest support, unprompted: "This project is reflexive (building the car)... process-level fussing is legitimate here in a way it is not on any other project... the reflexive project may still warrant more engine inspection than the amendment would give a driver." Despite this, the MODEL gate explicitly declares it "ran in driving mode" — applying reduced-engine-visibility defaults to itself, on this reflexive cycle, before ADR-101 existed to formally scope or authorize that application. The MODEL susceptibility snapshot then found exactly the failure modes one would predict from applying driver-mode defaults prematurely to a building-mode cycle: a concept (Cycle Posture) added and self-approved in the same authoring pass on "one metaphor at one gate"; domain vocabulary (which Invariant 0 reserves to the practitioner) treated as route/engine-level content; and a direct contradiction (Authority Resolution filed as an overridable "route" default while the cycle's own Essay-Outline calls it "not defaultable") that went unflagged to the practitioner. ADR-101 uses two of these same phase boundaries as its central grounding evidence ("the isolated check caught the triage's errors both times") without ever naming that the underlying premature self-application — driving-mode defaults on a building-mode cycle, ahead of this very ADR's acceptance — is itself a plausible cause of the errors being caught, not just a demonstration that the catch-mechanism works.

This also sharpens the "path 4" concern from Section 1: what actually happened at DISCOVER and MODEL is not "the mechanism this ADR proposes was operationalized and validated" so much as "an unratified, self-applied version of the mechanism produced the exact failure category (framing absorption on thin, self-authored evidence) that the sycophancy-resistance architecture (ADR-058 Tier 1; ADR-061 framing-audit-surfacing) exists to catch." Read this way, the evidence argues at least as strongly for tightening the pivot criterion or moving triage to an isolated context (ADR-101's own rejected Alternative D) as it does for "Accepted, not conditional."

One further inversion-relevant point: this argument audit's own findings are, per the dispatch brief, scheduled to be pre-triaged by the orchestrator under ADR-101's draft rule before reaching the practitioner. That makes this specific finding a live test of rule 4 ("framing-class findings are never silently resolved... surfaced with the agent's analysis done and a pivot extracted"). This finding is explicitly framing-class and should be surfaced in full, not summarized down to a disposition line.
