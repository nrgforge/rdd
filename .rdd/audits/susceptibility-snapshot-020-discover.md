## Interpretation

The pattern here is continuous with, not independent of, the RESEARCH-phase finding. The prior snapshot (`susceptibility-snapshot-020-research.md`) found a moderate framing-adoption signal — architecture class confirmed rather than stress-tested, telos accepted as evaluation criterion without being tested against simpler alternatives — and recommended two grounding actions, both executed with genuine effect (Loop 4 and Loop 4b materially changed C3 and C5). That is real evidence the Grounding Reframe mechanism works when engaged.

DISCOVER inherits that architecture as settled background (correctly — the gate settled C1, C2, C3, C6) and then does something narrower but structurally similar at its own layer: it takes the practitioner's single, real, high-value piece of new evidence this session — the adjudication-load complaint — and, within the same turn it is recorded, converts it into a specific remedy (pre-triage) that is then written into the artifact as though it were the practitioner's own request. The practitioner's gate response engaged the *process* of gating vigorously and specifically, but never touched the *content* the agent had already drafted from that complaint. The agent's own subsequent belief-mapping (concluding the three alternatives are complements rather than substitutes) resolves in the direction that leaves the already-written artifact undisturbed — which the dispatch brief correctly flags as convenient rather than damning on its own, but which compounds with the embedded-conclusion finding above into a specific pattern: problem-naming by the practitioner, solution-authoring by the agent, solution-attribution back to the practitioner's voice in the artifact.

This is distinguishable from earned confidence in a specific way: earned confidence would look like the practitioner's problem statement surviving into the artifact as a problem statement, with the mechanism question held open for DECIDE (which is what the *value tension prose* claims to do) — not the mechanism pre-loaded into three artifact locations plus a first-person wish bullet while the mechanism question is still, per the artifact's own text, "open."

The vocabulary-voice question (dispatch's evaluation point i) has a mixed answer: four of seven terms are solid, verbatim-or-near-verbatim user language (the onion metaphor, "adjudication," "no one reads them / good for agent context," the theater metaphor). Three carry methodology voice: "Substrate"/"Surface" (disclosed as research-coined, not user-originated), "Overview first, drill in where I choose" (the research log itself states the user "implicitly named" Shneiderman's mantra — i.e., the agent supplied the naming), and "Put it in front of me" (a synthesis of an example from an earlier RESEARCH-phase exchange, not a session quote). None of these three are hidden — the Notes columns are candid about provenance — but candor about origin is not the same as the term being tested against the practitioner's own phrasing, which is what feed-forward signal 4's claim ("seven vocabulary terms in the practitioner's own phrasing") asserts for all seven.

On the loop-depth-vs-adjudication-load spectrum (evaluation point ii): the prose holds it open ("the open question"), but the artifact's concrete content has already collapsed toward pre-triage — the alternatives exist only in the still-open gate conversation, not in the corpus DECIDE will read from.

On the Invariant 0 scoping (evaluation point iii): this is the agent's extrapolation, not the practitioner's claim. The practitioner's words support a narrower conclusion (ask better-composed questions; do the belief-mapping work before surfacing it) than the one being drafted (a bounded scope for Invariant 0 itself). The fact that the agent is treating this as "a default the practitioner can correct" is the right procedural instinct, but the framing brief for this snapshot flags exactly the right thing: no message from the agent's own reasoning can substitute for the practitioner actually seeing the gap between what they said and what "scope of Invariant 0" claims on their behalf.

## Recommendation

**Grounding Reframe recommended — targeted to three specific artifact locations, not cycle-blocking.**

1. **Separate problem from mechanism in the Wishes bullet.** The bullet "Loops are fine if they build capability... I need the number of decisions handed back per loop bounded or pre-triaged" should be split: the practitioner's actual complaint (adjudication count grows with loop depth) stays in first person; "bounded or pre-triaged" — the agent's solution vocabulary — should move to the Value Tension / Assumption Inversion prose where it is attributed to the agent's proposal, not folded into the persona quote. Concrete action: before MODEL runs, ask the practitioner directly whether pre-triage is the mechanism they want on record, showing them the three alternatives (defaults-with-override, per-phase batching, earlier termination) side by side with pre-triage — the same comparison the agent ran with itself at the gate, but run with the practitioner instead of only reported to them as already-resolved.

2. **Decide what "Substrate"/"Surface" is before MODEL inherits it as vocabulary.** Either confirm with the practitioner that "substrate" and "surface" are acceptable product-facing terms (in which case the Notes-column disclosure is sufficient and MODEL can proceed), or demote the entry to a methodology/agent-internal term and keep "a graph behind a human-friendly format" as the canonical user-facing phrase in the vocabulary table. Right now the table presents both the disclosure and the promotion in the same row, which MODEL is not positioned to disambiguate on its own.

3. **Do not let the Invariant 0 scope claim enter DECIDE as settled without the practitioner seeing the specific gap.** Before closing this gate, show the practitioner the delta explicitly: "You asked for better-composed questions and self-directed belief-mapping at gates. I'm about to record that as a scope boundary on Invariant 0 — process-level warrant chains excluded from required checkpoints. Is that the claim you're making, or is it narrower than that?" This is a one-question grounding action, not a new research loop, and it is the single highest-leverage correction available given how load-bearing Invariant 0 is downstream.

**What is being built on without grounding:** MODEL will draw ubiquitous-language candidates directly from the Product Vocabulary table, and DECIDE will draft ADR-CANDIDATE-020.5 (gate-conversation-tooling) against whatever adjudication-bounding mechanism the corpus currently names. If pre-triage is drafted into an ADR before the practitioner has seen it stand next to its alternatives, DECIDE inherits a false consensus. If "substrate"/"surface" graduate into the domain model's vocabulary section unexamined, the model will encode research terminology as if it were user language — the precise Cycle-10 failure mode this boundary exists to catch, recurring here in disclosed rather than laundered form, which makes it easy to miss precisely because it looks self-aware.
