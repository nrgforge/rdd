### From cycle entry (orchestrator to RESEARCH)

**1. Apply constraint-removal at research entry (ADR-082) with the existing RDD methodology as the named bracketed artifact.** This cycle's reflexivity makes the constraint-removal step structurally load-bearing: the artifact under critique is the methodology that runs the cycle. The first-mover-advantage problem ADR-082 addresses applies here with unusual force — the user has already framed a candidate diagnosis ("over-engineered systems by front-loading thorough processes"). The research-methods reviewer's pre-loop dispatch (Tier 1, ADR-060) should pay particular attention to whether the question's framing presupposes the conclusion, and whether the alternative ("RDD's structural posture is right; perceived over-engineering is methodology-mismatch") is held with comparable substantive depth.

**2. The question is REFLEXIVE.** The methodology that runs the research cycle is the methodology under investigation. This creates two specific risks the research phase must hold:
   - **Self-justification susceptibility**: the agent's training and the corpus's accumulated rationale may produce a research output that defends RDD without examining the critique substantively. Tier 1 architectural-isolation mechanisms (citation audit, argument audit with framing audit, research methods review, susceptibility snapshot) are the principal defenses; the user's pushback at the gate is the secondary defense.
   - **Self-flagellation susceptibility**: equally, the agent may over-correct toward the critique to demonstrate non-defensiveness, producing a research output that adopts the critique without examining its grounds. The framing audit at R1+ should test for both directions of asymmetric content selection.

**3. Existing partial answers in the corpus that must be engaged, not rediscovered.** The methodology has already produced concept-level answers to overlapping questions; the research phase should reference these directly and examine where they cover the over-engineering critique, where they don't, and where they may themselves be part of the problem:
   - **Reflection 005** "RDD as a Deep Work Tool" — methodology positioned as a tool reached for when problems warrant structured thinking and put away when knowledge has been absorbed. Frames RDD as a *scoped intervention*, not a permanent obligation.
   - **Invariant 4** (Cycle 5 amendment, domain-model.md:31) — "Epistemic cost must be productive, not merely brief." The constraint is on *waste*, not *duration*. Earned fatigue from deep engagement signals a break, not methodology-too-heavy.
   - **ADR-014** "Worth the Calories" quality gate — already encodes a quality-vs-cost-of-process tension as a gate criterion.
   - **ADR-083** Cognitive Economy Criterion (Cycle 017) — admissibility criterion for human-facing artifacts; the *Bolt-On / Felt Additional* signal as a failure-mode indicator; the *Outcome Test vs. Reading-Cost Test* tension; the *Orientation-About-Orientation* failure mode.
   - **ADR-091** Dual-Mode BUILD (Cycle 018) — auto-mode as a methodology-recognized response to overhead at the BUILD layer when work is mechanical and well-specified.
   - **Domain Model Open Question** (line 926): *"When does documentation fatigue signal 'time to graduate' versus 'the methodology is too heavy for this problem'?"* — already partially answered: the test is whether the work has shifted from identity-forming to feature-extending. This partial answer is a candidate sub-frame for the over-engineering question.
   - **Graduation lifecycle** — RDD's scoped-and-graduate design is itself a structural answer to "the methodology becomes too much over time": graduate, fold knowledge into native docs, archive scaffolding.

The research phase should treat these as **prior art**, citing where they answer the question and where they don't, rather than re-deriving them.

**4. Carry-forward from Cycle 018 BUILD feed-forward (potentially relevant).** Cycle 018's auto-mode BUILD observations name two BUILD-originated design decisions that surfaced as "inside the auto-mode failure-mode envelope" (Boundary 3 escalation rule; Boundary 2 routing crystallization). **5. The five most plausible competing diagnoses the research entry should hold open.** Constraint-removal should sweep these — not as a checklist, but as the space the question opens onto:
   - **Methodology-mismatch diagnosis**: RDD is calibrated for high-stakes/identity-forming work; perceived over-engineering arises when it's applied to lower-stakes work that doesn't warrant it. Solution: graduation lifecycle, scoped cycles, dual-mode mechanisms. (Existing partial answer.)
   - **Front-loading-amplifies-possibility-space diagnosis**: the user's stated framing. Thorough early-phase processes (research, discover, model, decide) elaborate the problem space in ways that propagate into over-built downstream artifacts (system design, code). Implication: research front-loading IS the problem mechanism.
   - **Audit-infrastructure-cost diagnosis**: the specialist subagent audits (citation, argument, framing, susceptibility-snapshot, research-methods-review, conformance) and their iterative-audit pattern (R1 → R2 → R3 → R4) carry substantial token and time cost. Cycle 018's BUILD feed-forward notes ~14 specialist subagents dispatched for one cycle. The over-engineering may be in the verification layer, not the construction layer.
   - **Possibility-space-vs-need-space diagnosis**: research opens a wide space of possibility (alternatives, prior art, methodology context); the user's actual need may be a small subset. RDD's discipline of "examine alternatives before deciding" may systematically over-elaborate vs. what the user would have settled on directly.
   - **Process-vs-product-conflation diagnosis**: the over-engineering perception may be at the methodology layer (the *artifacts* of the methodology, not the *system* being built). RDD's outputs — ADRs, scenarios, domain models, system designs, audit reports — may exceed what the system being designed warrants. The corpus produces more than the system needs, even when the system itself is right-sized.

**6. Research lit-review scope candidates.** Beyond RDD's internal corpus, the question opens onto established literatures the research phase may want to engage:

   - Possibility-space literature in design research (Cross, design space exploration).
   - Methodology-mismatch literature (Cockburn's "Crystal" methodology-fit framework; situated methodology selection).
