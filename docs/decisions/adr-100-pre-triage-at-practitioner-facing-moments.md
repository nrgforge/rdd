## Context

The cycle's RESEARCH remedy (ADR-099) and its gate-tooling complement both reduce the cost of reading each item the practitioner is handed. Neither reduces how many items there are. DISCOVER surfaced the distinction from the practitioner's own report on returning after three months: *"Process time is ok and looping is ok if it increases capability and drives results. However what tends to happen is that loops peel back more layers of the onion that need more adjudication by the reader. So it's exhausting."* The domain model names this Adjudication Load: the count of decisions handed back per loop or audit round, growing with loop depth, distinct from reading load. The methodology's existing bounds (ADR-094 on audit rounds; ADR-096 on research loops) limit rounds and loops, not items per round.

At the DISCOVER gate the practitioner named the gate protocol itself as part of the load: *"the belief inversion 'what would you need to believe' type questions often get surfaced to me in these reflection time moments, but what I really need is for the agent to ask itself this question. Come to me with real pivot points... The user here is not going to really know the very fine-grained detail of the process being orchestrated. But they should understand product direction, their goal, the outcome they want."* Asked for Invariant 0's text, the practitioner ruled that the invariant stands as written (authority over what was built, for whom, and why) and that gate practice under Invariants 1 and 2 had drifted toward process-level questions the invariant never required. The practitioner's metaphor: RDD is a car; on any project other than this one the practitioner is driving, decides where to go and sometimes whether to take the highway or the scenic route, and does not need the internal machinations explained. The practitioner added that this project is the exception: it is building the car, and fussing over how it works is legitimate here. At the DECIDE gate the practitioner added a fact that bears on what a gate ruling is: they have authored none of the corpus's 98 ADRs; all were composed by agents in session. The practitioner's contribution to a decision record has been the ruling and the verbatim statements the record carries, which is where Invariant 3 places the epistemic act. This ADR does not treat that fact as showing any particular ruling was adequate under Invariant 1; it treats it as showing what the ruling is the practitioner's act on.

**Evidentiary character of this ADR.** Unlike ADR-099, this decision has no RESEARCH-phase trail. Adjudication load and pre-triage surfaced at the DISCOVER resumption, were carried through MODEL by the agent's own self-run belief-mapping, and arrive at DECIDE grounded by stakeholder-articulated demand (ADR-097 path 2) and by what happened at three in-cycle gates. That in-cycle record cuts both ways and both halves are stated here. At the DISCOVER and MODEL boundaries the orchestrator pre-triaged the susceptibility snapshot's findings and brought one pivot each; at both boundaries the isolated snapshot caught what the triage had got wrong: mechanism vocabulary written into the practitioner's voice, research terms under a user label, an invariant scoping the practitioner had not made, a concept committed on one metaphor, a "not defaultable" choice filed as a default. The snapshots' own reading is that the pattern was intensifying and that MODEL ran in a reduced-visibility mode on this reflexive cycle before any ADR existed to scope that mode. So the two boundaries are evidence that in-context triage errs (the harm direction) and that an isolated reader of the triage record catches it (the reliance direction). A third boundary, this ADR's own DECIDE gate, added the sharpest instance: the orchestrator's triage record claimed two R1 findings (the pivot-criterion gloss; the absence of a RESEARCH trail behind this ADR) had been put to the practitioner when the presentation contained neither, which is rule 4 below violated on its first application, caught by the decide-boundary snapshot reading the record. None of this is BUILD-phase validation and this ADR does not claim ADR-097 path 4. The status is Conditional for that reason, and the graduation trigger counts both directions, with Cycle 020 as the first cycle in the tally.

## Decision

At every practitioner-facing moment where the methodology hands findings or questions to the practitioner (research-loop ends, audit-round ends, framing-audit surfacing, susceptibility-snapshot grounding actions, and the AID gate itself), the orchestrator **pre-triages** before presenting, and presents what survives in **plain language with referenced content inline**.

Two mechanisms are decided here as one practitioner-facing channel, and they map to the domain model's two components of Corpus Overwhelm: **rules 1 through 5 address Adjudication Load** (item count); **rules 6 and 7 address reading load** (per-item cost), complementing ADR-099's Surface Views, which address reading load for the corpus rather than the conversation. They are held in one ADR because they fire at the same moments and are inspected by the same record; the mapping is stated so that a reader tracing either remedy finds it.

### Pre-Triage (Adjudication Load)

1. **Sort every finding into one of three dispositions.** *Resolve:* the agent can and does act on it, and records what it did. *Carry forward:* it does not need a ruling now; it is recorded in cycle-status or the next phase's inputs with the phase that will take it up. *Pivot:* it changes the destination, and the practitioner rules.
2. **Pivot criterion.** A finding is a Pivot Point when it changes the destination: what gets built, who it is built for, or why. Route-level choices (how to get there) are presented as overridable defaults with the agent's choice stated; the practitioner overrides or does not. Engine-level detail (how the process is orchestrated) is resolved by the agent and logged. The destination / route / engine tiering is the practitioner's own (the car metaphor); the mapping of "destination" onto Invariant 0's list is the agent's gloss, is put to the practitioner at the Cycle 020 DECIDE gate for confirmation, and is the first thing the snapshot's triage evaluation checks.
3. **The agent runs the question form on itself first.** For each Pivot Point the agent performs the belief-mapping, rebuttal elicitation, or warrant elicitation ADR-056 prescribes for the phase, develops both sides with comparable depth, states its recommendation, and hands the practitioner the one ruling that remains. The practitioner's epistemic act under Invariants 1 and 2 is that ruling, not the process-level analysis behind it.
4. **Framing-class findings are never silently resolved.** A framing-audit or snapshot finding that names an alternative the artifact did not choose is surfaced with the agent's analysis done and a pivot extracted. It may not be dispositioned "resolve" without the practitioner seeing it. This preserves the framing-audit-surfacing rule (ADR-061) and is the boundary between pre-triage and content selection.
5. **The triage record is written, not implied.** At a gate, the gate reflection note (ADR-066) gains a required section, "Susceptibility snapshot findings and orchestrator triage," holding a table of finding, disposition, and what was done. At a non-gate moment the record is appended to the artifact the moment produced, under a heading "Findings and orchestrator triage": the research log's loop-end section for a research loop; the end of the audit report for an audit round; the snapshot file itself for grounding actions. The record travels with its source and is what the next isolated evaluator reads. A pre-triage with no record is a Tier 2 conversational mechanism and does not satisfy this ADR.

### Plain-language presentation (reading load)

6. **Referenced content is inlined at the point of use.** A gate question, pivot, or default that cites an invariant, ADR section, scenario, or vocabulary term quotes the cited content or a faithful one-line gloss inline. Bare identifiers ("Invariant 9 plus ADR-014 §C.4 implies") are not presented to the practitioner. This is the Chandler and Sweller (1992) split-attention remedy at the gate, and the practitioner's "plain language instead of cross-referential subsection-numbering."
7. **One screen, one ruling.** The presentation of a phase's output is one screen of what changed, the route defaults taken, and the pivot. It composes with the one-question-at-a-time convention.

### Where the isolation lives

Pre-triage runs in the orchestrator's own context. That is content selection by the party whose selection is under audit (Cheng et al. 2026; ADR-058). The structural answer is not to move triage into a subagent for every moment (see rejected alternative D) but to make the triage record the input to the isolated susceptibility snapshot at the next phase boundary, which already runs unconditionally (ADR-065). The snapshot brief for each phase is amended to evaluate the triage record explicitly: were pivots correctly identified, were framing-class findings surfaced, did any "resolve" disposition hide a destination change. The product-discovery value tension this ADR answers ("whether agent triage preserves the practitioner's authority or erodes it") stays open in the sense that matters: the record plus the isolated reader is how the question is kept answerable, cycle by cycle.

### Posture: driving, building, and what this cycle did

- **Design target is driving.** A practitioner applying RDD to a project decides destination and, when they care, route, and never reads the engine. The rules above are specified for that practitioner.
- **A reflexive cycle may choose building.** A cycle applying RDD to itself may opt into engine-level presentation at cycle open, or at any gate, by saying so; engine-level dispositions are then listed in the presentation, not only logged.
- **What this cycle did, stated plainly.** Cycle 020 is reflexive. Its DISCOVER gate noted the reflexive project might warrant more engine visibility than a driver. Its MODEL phase nonetheless ran in reduced-visibility mode, on the practitioner's DISCOVER-gate instruction to bring pivots rather than process, before this ADR existed to define the mode or its opt-out. The MODEL snapshot found problems consistent with that: a concept self-approved on one metaphor; vocabulary treated as engine; a not-defaultable choice filed as a default. This ADR treats the practitioner's instruction as the opt-in it functionally was. At the DECIDE gate (2026-09-03) the driving-or-building question for the rest of the cycle was presented as one of two stated defaults and was not overridden, which the gate record marks as weaker than a ruling; the question was put on its own at the ARCHITECT gate (2026-09-04), where the practitioner ruled driving with disclosure: snapshot-caught triage errors are shown in the presentation, and engine-level dispositions stay logged in the triage record.

### Scope, tier, and grounding

- **Tier: Skill-Structure Layer is the tier this ADR specifies; the anchoring step ships in BUILD.** The triage record is a required section of the gate reflection note produced by each phase skill's gate, and the presentation rule amends the AID cycle text in the orchestrator and each phase skill's gate step. No skill text yet contains that step (conformance scan, 2026-09-03); until BUILD writes it, the mechanism is practiced by the orchestrator (this cycle's three gate notes carry the section) and is Tier 2 in Invariant 8's sense. The record, not a hook, is chosen as the anchor because the record *is* the mechanism: without it there is nothing for the isolated reader to read, and the cost of a required table is low. What waits for multi-cycle evidence is hook enforcement of the record's presence and shape, which is a higher-cost, higher-risk commitment (a manifest check that can block or nag). The two evidentiary bars differ because the two commitments differ in cost and risk, not because the evidence is read differently.
- **Grounding (ADR-097):** stakeholder-articulated demand (path 2), the strongest the cycle has. The in-cycle boundary record is operational evidence in both directions, not path 4. No RESEARCH-phase grounding exists; the narrow literature that would supply it (management by exception; escalation and hand-off policies in human-automation teaming; triage in human-AI decision systems) is named here as the grounding action for the Conditional period, to be run as a targeted lit-review or spike before or during BUILD, not as a blocking research loop.
- **Status: Conditional Acceptance.** Consistent with ADR-099's treatment of stronger evidence and with ADR-097 and ADR-098's two-to-four-cycle bar.
- **Graduation trigger.** Two directions, per ADR-098; stated in §Graduation trigger below.

### Graduation trigger

The trigger counts cycles, not boundaries. Cycle 020's three boundaries are cycle one and already register in both directions: harm (three triage errors, one a rule-4 violation) and reliance (all three caught by the isolated reader). They predate the rules' formal adoption, and the R2 audit suggested excluding them on that ground; they are counted anyway because the mechanism they ran is the one this ADR specifies, and an ADR whose own in-cycle record did not count against it would be setting the bar it is measured by. *Reliance evidence:* if two to four cycles' snapshots catch triage errors that would have changed the destination, the Harness Layer adds a manifest check that the triage section exists and is non-empty, and the status transitions to Accepted. *Harm evidence:* if two to four cycles' snapshots find pivots consistently missed under "resolve" dispositions and the practitioner does not pursue the grounding action, the pivot criterion is tightened or triage moves to an isolated context (alternative D). *Relaxation:* if snapshots find the triage clean across two to four cycles, the record stays, the status transitions to Accepted, and the snapshot brief's triage evaluation is reduced to a spot check.

**Warrants.**

- *(commitment (rules 1–5))* Stakeholder-articulated demand (ADR-097 path 2): "loops peel back more layers of the onion that need more adjudication by the reader. So it's exhausting" (practitioner, DISCOVER resumption 2026-09-03). The pain is item count, which ADR-099 and the gate tooling do not reduce.
- *(commitment (rules 2–3))* The practitioner's DISCOVER-gate instruction: "what I really need is for the agent to ask itself this question. Come to me with real pivot points"; and the ruling that Invariant 0 stands as written while gate practice had drifted toward process-level questions the invariant never required.
- *(commitment (rule 5 and §Where the isolation lives))* In-cycle boundary record in both directions: at the DISCOVER, MODEL, and DECIDE boundaries the orchestrator's in-context triage erred (harm direction) and the isolated snapshot reading the triage record caught it (reliance direction); the DECIDE boundary was a rule-4 violation on first application. The ARCHITECT boundary added a fourth instance (a miscount inside a resolve disposition).
- *(commitment (rule 6))* Split-attention effect (Chandler and Sweller 1992): a gate question that cites content elsewhere forces cold lookup; inlining the referenced content at the point of use is the remedy the practitioner named as "plain language instead of cross-referential subsection-numbering."
- *(commitment (§Where the isolation lives; rejected alternative D))* Pre-triage is content selection by the party under audit (Cheng et al. 2026; ADR-058). The structural answer that stays within the cycle's latency budget is to make the triage record the input to the isolated snapshot that already runs unconditionally (ADR-065).
- *(status (Conditional Acceptance))* No RESEARCH-phase trail exists for this ADR (framing audit R1 P1-b); the named literature (management by exception; escalation and hand-off in human-automation teaming; triage in human-AI decision systems) is the grounding action for the Conditional period.

## Rejected alternatives

**A. The practitioner adjudicates every finding (status quo).** Every audit finding, framing finding, and grounding action is presented raw and the practitioner rules. Rejected on the strongest evidence in the cycle: the practitioner stopped using the methodology. Invariant 1 requires the practitioner to produce something at a gate; it does not require that the something be a ruling on process detail, and Invariant 0's list is the destination, not the engine.

**B. Earlier termination only.** Cut adjudication count by cutting loops and rounds. ADR-094 and ADR-096 already do this at the round and loop level. Rejected as the sole mechanism because the practitioner explicitly wants loops that increase capability, and termination cuts capability with count.

**C. Per-phase batching only.** Hold all findings until the phase boundary and present them once. Rejected as the sole mechanism because it lowers interrupt frequency, not item count; it is a presentation choice rule 7 already adopts.

**D. Triage in an isolated subagent at every practitioner-facing moment.** Architecturally clean: the party selecting content is not the party whose content is audited. Rejected for this cycle because it adds a dispatch at every loop end and audit round (latency the practitioner said is acceptable only when it drives results), and because the compound already in place (orchestrator triage plus isolated snapshot reading the triage record) caught the triage's errors at all three boundaries this cycle. The third boundary is D's strongest evidence, not the incumbent's: the orchestrator misreported its own triage record, and only an isolated reader caught it. Named as the harm-evidence graduation target: the in-cycle record shows the errors were real, and if snapshots show in-context triage systematically hides pivots, this is the next move. This is the closest call in the ADR, and the DECIDE boundary moved it closer.

**E. Silent triage.** Pre-triage with no written record. Rejected because it violates Invariant 8 (an unanchored mechanism does not fire reliably) and the framing-audit-surfacing rule, and because it is indistinguishable from the sycophantic content selection the Tier 1 mechanisms exist to catch. The record is the mechanism.

**F. Defaults-with-override as the only mechanism.** Present every finding as a default the practitioner may override. Rejected as the sole mechanism because it is a presentation form of triage, not a count reducer: the practitioner still reads every default. It is retained as the route-level presentation in rule 2.

**G. Amend Invariant 0 to exclude process-level detail.** The orchestrator's first reading of the practitioner's gate response. Rejected by the practitioner after reading the invariant's text: the invariant already scopes authority to what was built, for whom, and why. Gate practice, not the invariant, is what changes.

**H. Split into two ADRs (pre-triage; plain-language presentation).** The domain model assigns the two mechanisms to different components of Corpus Overwhelm, and one-decision-per-ADR would put them in separate files. Rejected because they fire at the same moments, are inspected through the same record, and would each be a thin ADR whose only reader is the agent; the explicit rule-to-concept mapping above gives a reader what the split would have given without the second file. Recorded as a route default at the DECIDE gate.

**I. Hold as `adr-deferred-` pending a RESEARCH loop.** The framing audit noted this ADR has no RESEARCH trail. Rejected because the mechanism is already operating (this cycle's gates), the stakeholder demand is direct, and the cost of the mechanism is a table; Conditional Acceptance with the named grounding action is the ADR-097 form for "commit now, validate on the way." The alternative is recorded so that a future reader knows it was the closer of the two forms.

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Adjudication Load is bounded at the source: the practitioner sees pivots and route defaults, not the full finding set.
- The gate's epistemic act is realigned with Invariant 0's list. The practitioner rules on destination.
- The triage record makes the orchestrator's content selection inspectable, which is the only way an in-context selection can be audited.
- Gate prompts stop requiring cold lookup.
- The framing-audit-surfacing rule is preserved and sharpened: surfaced with analysis, not raw.

**Negative:**
- Triage is content selection by the orchestrator. The isolated snapshot at the next boundary is the check, and it runs once per phase, so a pivot hidden under "resolve" mid-phase is caught late. This cycle's own record shows the errors are real. The graduation trigger names the escalation.
- The pivot criterion's mapping onto Invariant 0's list is the agent's gloss on the practitioner's metaphor and gatekeeps what reaches the practitioner, including findings about itself. It is put to the practitioner at this gate and is the first check in every snapshot's triage evaluation.
- Reflexive cycles lose engine-level visibility by default and must opt in. This cycle's MODEL phase is the cautionary instance.
- No RESEARCH-phase grounding. The named grounding action is a debt this ADR carries.
- ADR-056 and ADR-066 are partially updated; their downstream artifacts need the sweep.

**Neutral:**
- The Question Toolkit's forms are unchanged. What changes is who runs the first pass.
- The one-question-at-a-time convention (practitioner feedback, Cycle 020 RESEARCH) is now encoded rather than remembered.
