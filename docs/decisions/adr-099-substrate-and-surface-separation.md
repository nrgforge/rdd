# ADR-099: Substrate-and-Surface Separation as Methodology-Wide Architecture

**Status:** Accepted (Conditional Acceptance: validation pending BUILD-phase operationalization at corpus scale, per ADR-097 path 4). The Authority sub-decision (§Authority) was ruled by the practitioner at the Cycle 020 DECIDE gate on 2026-09-03: option C. Accepted.
**Cycle:** 020 (Corpus Topology and Agent-Navigable Compaction)
**Audit trail:** R1 argument audit (`.rdd/audits/argument-audit-decide-020.md`) P1-1, P2-2, P2-3, P3-2 integrated 2026-09-03; the authority decision, drafted as ADR-100 at R1, was folded into this ADR as a nested sub-decision per the framing audit's one-ADR finding and this ADR's own constraint 4.

## Context

Cycle 020 opened on the practitioner's "uneven topology" hypothesis from the Cycle 019 DECIDE gate: five ADRs (094 through 098) that read as middle-of-the-road choices where one bold choice would be easier to validate. RESEARCH confirmed the hypothesis within the cycle (Essay-Outline 020 C1: the five ADRs apply one Calibration-with-Graduation-Trigger Pattern at five workflow points and compress to one Pattern record plus five entries without audit-substance loss) and then found the underlying problem is not ADR granularity. It is that one artifact serves two audiences with different needs. The agent needs the granular record with provenance. The human needs to see the corpus at the resolution the moment calls for, and today cannot: product-discovery.md is 235 KB, domain-model.md is 366 KB, scenarios.md is 274 KB, and a returning practitioner reads the agent's version or nothing.

The mechanism is Sweller's element interactivity (C2): the count of mutually-referencing artifacts that must be held at once determines the load, and the load operates at every holistic-synthesis moment (gates, architecture review, re-entry after time away, graduation), not at graduation alone. The practitioner's lived report generalizes (Lethbridge, Singer, and Forward 2003; Forward and Lethbridge 2002): decision documentation is task-triggered reading for humans and retained value for agents. Gupta et al. (2026) extend the asymmetry to the agent side: all-history delivery degrades agent output; a recency window plus structured traversal is better than the flat corpus.

DISCOVER added the strongest empirical signal the cycle has. After running Cycles 017 through 019, the practitioner stepped away for roughly three months and reported using RDD much less: *"it takes a long time and it's a lot of text to read."* This is stakeholder-articulated demand (ADR-097 grounding path 2). The RESEARCH spike (research log §Loop 5; spike-020) is the second grounding path: on the Cycle 019 five-ADR sample, a plain-JSON typed-link substrate with a derivation script produced an overview plus six drill-in Surface Views at 49% fewer lines with the audit-substance intact, and surfaced six design constraints any commitment must honor (C4).

Six prior ADRs (064, 083, 084, 085, 092, 095) each addressed the human/agent audience split at one point: category naming, human-facing outcome test, agent-context placement, infrastructure relocation, research-artifact form, agent-facing admissibility. Under the convergent reading they were building toward this commitment. Under the alternative reading, strengthened by the post-gate cross-cycle evidence (Cycles 017 and 018 do not show Cycle 019's clustering), they were complete answers to different problems and Cycle 020 names a new one: the read pattern itself, "navigate N artifacts," has never changed. This ADR proceeds under the alternative reading. Per Essay-Outline E5.4.3, that reading carries less inherited warrant and calls for a lighter tier or narrower scope than the convergent reading would; §Scope and tier below states which of the two it narrows and why.

## Decision

The methodology adopts **Substrate-and-Surface Separation** as a methodology-wide architectural commitment.

### The architecture

- **Substrate.** A layer of structured records, one per governance unit (ADR, invariant, pattern, vocabulary term, scenario block, and further types as they prove necessary), carrying the unit's audit-substance and its provenance via Typed Links to other records. The Substrate is agent-facing primarily and is treated as a first-class auditable layer: the argument auditor and citation auditor audit records, not only prose.
- **Surface View.** A view of the corpus computed from the Substrate at a chosen resolution: an overview first, drill-in on demand, with surrounding records kept visible at drill-in (focus+context, Cockburn et al. 2008) and the view's own incompleteness signaled. Surface Views are the human-facing read of the corpus and are never hand-maintained.
- **Single-source derivation.** A derivation tool, itself a methodology toolchain artifact, generates every Surface View from the Substrate in one deterministic pass. Corrections go to the Substrate; the view is regenerated. This is what prevents the dual-maintenance drift that any two-layer system produces by default. The mitigation is architectural, not disciplinary.

### Binding design constraints (from the spike, C4)

These are the "substrate entries" of this decision: constraints the architecture must honor, each with the alternative it rejects, so that folding them here preserves the audit-bearing shape C1 found necessary (Option A) rather than the compression that loses it (Options B and C).

1. **Extensible link schema.** Any string is a valid Typed Link type. *Rejected alternative:* a fixed type set. The spike's four starting types covered roughly half the relationships five ADRs needed; six more surfaced during authoring. A fixed schema is a Procrustean bed (also rejected alternative D below).
2. **Note Discipline.** Every non-trivial Typed Link carries note text stating what the relationship is. Schema validation rejects an unnoted non-trivial link. *Rejected alternative:* notes optional or recommended. The spike showed the type alone is not semantic payload and that absence fails silently: ADR-097's unnoted links to Invariants 0, 4, and 8 remained cold-lookup-required. *Considered and rejected:* filing Note Discipline as its own ADR (Essay-Outline §9 ADR-CANDIDATE-020.3). A validation rule with one warrant and one alternative is a substrate entry, not a decision with its own lifecycle.
3. **Per-type record schemas.** Pattern, ADR, Invariant, Vocabulary-term, and Scenario records have different minimal field sets. Optional fields are permitted; filling them for completeness rather than content is the failure mode to resist. Which types get schemas in this cycle is an ARCHITECT decision; the spike's finding is that at least Pattern, ADR, and Vocabulary-term differ materially. *Rejected alternative:* a universal record shape, which reproduces the arc42 and C4 comprehensiveness trap (rejected alternative D).
4. **Sub-argument structure is preserved or its loss is declared.** The spike's flat five-field block lost ADR-094's mechanism specification, ADR-097's Target 2 and Target 3 sub-decisions, and ADR-098's reflexive-application argument. ADR records permit nested sub-commitments and warrants tagged by what they warrant. Where a record type accepts compression loss, the schema says so and the Surface View marks the record as a compressed index, not a faithful encoding. *Rejected alternative:* accept flat compression uniformly and treat the Substrate as an index. Rejected because the loss is exactly the kind that becomes invisible once the source is no longer consulted (spike §1), and because this ADR itself relies on nesting to hold its Authority sub-decision. *Considered and rejected:* filing per-type schemas and nesting as their own ADR (ADR-CANDIDATE-020.4). They are format constraints ARCHITECT specifies against, not decisions with independent warrant.
5. **Authority is resolved explicitly, in §Authority below.** The spike defaulted to prose-canonical and found the single-source claim collapses under that default. The Essay-Outline marks the choice not defaultable; it is nested here as a sub-decision with its own options and its own ruling, per constraint 4.
6. **Authoring overhead is a toolchain concern.** Authoring five records was tractable. Authoring at corpus scale (roughly 100 ADRs) is untested. The Author Substrate Record action is part of the methodology's artifact-creation steps, and the derivation tool is a maintained artifact. *Rejected alternative:* a one-off transformation script. Rejected because a two-layer system without a maintained derivation tool decays into the dual-maintenance failure single-source derivation exists to prevent.

### Authority (sub-decision; Accepted, option C, practitioner's ruling 2026-09-03)

Single-source derivation requires a single source. The options, developed for the practitioner's ruling because the choice determines what the practitioner edits from now on:

- **A. Substrate canonical everywhere, including retroactive migration of legacy units.** The cleanest single-source story. Set aside because authoring roughly 100 records is the untested scaling case (constraint 6), because migration is interpretive and would silently lose sub-argument structure across the whole history (spike §7), and because re-encoding accepted bodies contradicts ADR-074. If BUILD shows authoring is cheap at scale, a later ADR can promote legacy units wholesale.
- **B. Source prose canonical forever; Substrate as a derived index.** Preserves the prose-authoring habit, whose strongest merit is that a rejected-alternatives section in prose forces substantive engagement. Set aside because the spike ran this default and found it collapses single-source derivation (every prose edit needs interpretive re-encoding), because it keeps the practitioner writing 400-line artifacts whose only reader after graduation is the agent, and because the Substrate becomes a permanent second maintenance target. Its strongest merit is answered by making rejected-alternatives-with-reasons a required field of the ADR record type (constraint 3), so the engagement is a schema requirement rather than a prose habit. At the DECIDE gate the practitioner added the fact that settles it for this corpus: they have authored none of the 98 ADRs; all were composed by agents in session. The prose habit's merit was the agent's analytical engagement, never the practitioner's, and a required field preserves it for the agent as prose does. That is all this fact establishes here: it removes B's strongest argument. It does not by itself establish that the practitioner's ruling was an adequate epistemic act under Invariant 1; that question belongs to the gate record and the susceptibility snapshot, not to this ADR. Scope of the audit-substance claim: in this corpus audit-substance is read by auditors and by the orchestrator; the practitioner reads it only through Surface Views. A required field preserves it for those readers.
- **C. Substrate canonical for units authored after this ADR; legacy units canonical for themselves, represented by index records. (Recommended.)** New governance units are authored as Substrate records via Author Substrate Record; the record is the immutable body in ADR-074's sense; the markdown ADR at the existing path is a derived Surface View carrying a header that names the record and is overwritten on regeneration. Legacy units (ADRs 001 through 098, prior essays, existing domain-model prose) remain canonical in their files. Each gets an index record: identifier, title, status, Typed Links with notes, and a summary marked compressed (constraint 4). The spike's interpretive re-encoding problem does not recur for legacy units because their bodies are frozen under ADR-074: only status metadata changes (supersession, deprecation), and those updates are mechanical. What does recur once is the initial authoring of roughly 98 index records, which is an instance of constraint 6 and is BUILD's to measure. Promotion of a legacy unit to a full record is permitted when a cycle touches it substantively (a supersession; a Cycle 019-style compression to a Pattern record plus entries) and is never required. This follows ADR-092's precedent for legacy essays: the form change applies going forward and the corpus mixes forms.
- **D. Dual canonical by role: prose ADRs for history, Substrate for current state.** Attractive because it maps onto the historical-record versus current-state distinction the DECIDE skill already draws. Set aside for two reasons developed at comparable depth. First, audit-substance (rejected alternatives, thresholds, triggers) is historical content, so under D it lives in the prose ADR while its typed links and status live in the Substrate, which puts one unit's content on two update paths with two authoring practices: the dual-maintenance failure single-source derivation exists to prevent. Second, the current-state artifacts D would assign to the Substrate (system-design, ORIENTATION) are already Surface Views in this ADR's terms, generated from records; making the Substrate itself a current-state form adds a third layer without removing one, and leaves the question of what the derivation tool derives *from* unanswered.
- **E. Defer to ARCHITECT or BUILD.** Set aside because the choice is about what the practitioner edits, which is DECIDE's, and because the spike showed what defaulting does. Format is ARCHITECT's; authority is not.

The practitioner ruled C at the DECIDE gate (2026-09-03; response: "C"). ADR-074 carries a dated `Updated by ADR-099` header stating that for units authored after this ADR the immutable body is the Substrate record and the markdown file is derived.

**Nested sub-decision status (defining the pattern ADR-074 did not).** A sub-decision nested in an ADR per constraint 4 may carry its own status only while the ADR is in DECIDE. It must be ruled before the ADR's gate closes; a sub-decision that would remain open across a gate is filed as its own ADR instead. Once the gate closes, the outer status is the only status, and the nested ruling is part of the immutable body. This is what happened here: §Authority was Proposed for the span of one gate and was ruled at it.

**Why the ADR-074 downstream sweep is deferred without opening the drift window ADR-074 rejects.** ADR-074 rejects deferring the current-state sweep because a superseded decision leaves stale text in system-design, ORIENTATION, and field-guide until the sweep runs. That is not the situation here. No current-state artifact yet describes a Substrate, a derived ADR view, or a record-as-body; the sweep for this update is the *creation* of that content, which cannot precede the toolchain that gives it meaning, not the *correction* of text that has gone stale. The drift window is empty until BUILD writes the first record. ARCHITECT states where the content will land; BUILD writes it; the deferral is recorded in cycle-status. The downstream sweep (system-design, ORIENTATION, field-guide) is deferred to BUILD, when the toolchain makes the distinction operational; the deferral is recorded in cycle-status.

### Complement, not substitute

Gate-conversation tooling that inlines referenced content at the point of use (the practitioner's "plain language instead of cross-referential subsection-numbering") addresses gate-time split attention (Chandler and Sweller 1992) well and cheaply. It does not provide multi-resolution viewing, graduation consolidation, or agent-context delivery (research log §Grounding Loop 4b). It ships as part of ADR-100's presentation rule, alongside this architecture, not instead of it. Against the domain model's decomposition of Corpus Overwhelm: this ADR's Surface Views and ADR-100's rules 6 and 7 both address reading load (per-item cost); ADR-100's rules 1 through 5 address Adjudication Load (item count). This ADR does not address Adjudication Load.

### Scope and tier

The alternative reading of the lineage carries less inherited warrant than the convergent reading (E5.4.3). This ADR narrows the **tier**, not the **scope**, and states why:

- **Scope: methodology-wide.** Scope does not rest on the lineage reading. It rests on C2: element interactivity is a structural mechanism that operates at every holistic-synthesis moment, and the Essay-Outline (§1 META) locates the methodology-wide warrant in the literature's mechanism plus single-cycle corpus evidence, not in cross-cycle topology data. A graduation-scoped or cycle-internal architecture would leave the mid-cycle moments C2 established untouched. This corpus is the first instance and the validation target.
- **Tier: Tier-2 architectural commitment now; Skill-Structure Layer anchoring is the graduation target.** This is the narrowing the alternative reading calls for. The commitment is to the architecture and the six constraints; no skill step is amended by this ADR. When BUILD delivers the toolchain and it runs on this corpus, the Author Substrate Record action becomes a concrete step in the DECIDE and MODEL skills at artifact-creation moments and Derive Surface View becomes a step at phase exit. Until then, nothing structurally forces a record to be authored, and the ADR says so.
- **Status: Conditional Acceptance.** The status hedge and the tier narrowing are different axes and both apply. Grounding (ADR-097): stakeholder-articulated demand (path 2) and spike validation (path 3, five records). Corpus-scale validation is structural operationalization (path 4) and is what transitions this ADR to full Acceptance and the tier to Skill-Structure. If BUILD at corpus scale surfaces friction the spike did not (cross-cycle link consistency, provenance link rot, namespace collision, cycle-archive interaction), the constraints are revised by a superseding ADR, not by editing this one.
- **Design target:** a practitioner driving RDD on a project, who decides destination and, when they care, route, and never reads the engine. See ADR-100 for the posture question this cycle's own gates raised.

### Held caveat (C7)

The architecture inherits the kitchen-sink risk ADR-097 names for the Calibration-with-Graduation-Trigger Pattern: its diagnostic value depends on producing actionable distinctions at artifact-creation moments, not on being retrospectively applicable to any artifact. The cross-cycle topology evidence is single-cycle at the clustering level. Multi-cycle observation per ADR-097 and ADR-098's graduation-trigger lineage is the response.

### Graduation trigger

After BUILD operationalizes the toolchain on this corpus: if two to four cycles author Substrate records at DECIDE and the derived Surface Views are what the practitioner actually reads at gates and on re-entry (observable: gate notes and cycle-status cite views, not raw artifacts), the Conditional Acceptance transitions to Accepted and the Skill-Structure steps are confirmed. If the practitioner continues to read raw artifacts, or authoring overhead is reported as the new corpus-overwhelm, the architecture is re-examined: the likely revision is scope (which record types) rather than the separation itself.

## Rejected alternatives

**A. Gate-conversation tooling only.** Inline the referenced invariants and ADR sections at gate prompts; change nothing else. Rejected as insufficient, not as wrong: Grounding Loop 4b showed it addresses the specific gate-friction example and none of multi-resolution viewing, graduation consolidation, or agent-context delivery. It ships as a complement in ADR-100.

**B. Thematic consolidation at graduation.** Scaffold the practitioner's existing practice (consolidate ADRs by theme when graduating a corpus). Rejected because consolidation produces a flat thematic document: it does not produce overview-to-drill-in navigation, and Loop 1.3 showed the compression either loses audit-substance (Option B) or fails to compact (Option C). It also leaves every mid-cycle synthesis moment untouched, and C2 established those share the mechanism.

**C. A knowledge-graph database as the substrate.** The practitioner's entry hypothesis; the plexus MCP is available in this environment. Rejected on two grounds. GraphReader (EMNLP 2024) shows the navigation gain comes from graph-structured traversal, not from a stored graph artifact, and Volpini et al. (2026) find typed-link affordances at the plain-text level capture most of the retrieval gain. And the plugin-native constraint (no external service in the methodology's toolchain) rules out a database dependency. Tool availability is not warrant.

**D. A fixed universal record schema.** Rejected by the spike directly: the starting schema under-covered on contact, and the Pattern record stretched the five-field block. Constraints 1 and 3 encode the rejection.

**E. Corpus pruning.** Delete or archive artifacts to reduce weight. Rejected because the practitioner's constraint-removal response at RESEARCH entry made audit-substance preservation binding, and because the agent retains value from the granular records (Forward and Lethbridge 2002; Gupta et al. 2026). The problem is who has to read them, not that they exist.

**F. Defer the architecture; ship only what is cheap.** Ship ADR-100 now and hold this ADR as `adr-deferred-`. Rejected because the stakeholder-articulated demand is for the corpus the practitioner does not have to read, which only the architecture delivers, and because the spike is already the grounding a deferred ADR would wait for. Conditional Acceptance with Tier-2 anchoring is the honest form: commit the architecture, validate at scale, anchor in skill text when the toolchain exists.

**G. Retroactive full-corpus migration in this cycle.** Author records for all existing ADRs now. Rejected because authoring at that scale is untested (constraint 6) and because §Authority option C makes migration unnecessary. The Cycle 019 five-ADR sample plus this cycle's own ADRs are the first corpus.

**H. One ADR for the whole cycle, with pre-triage nested here as well.** The framing audit (R1) noted the cycle's own C1 finding and the practitioner's stated preference for one higher-level ADR point toward a single ADR covering both remedies for Corpus Overwhelm. Rejected because ADR-100's remedy has a different evidence base (no RESEARCH trail; stakeholder demand at DISCOVER), a different lifecycle (Conditional pending multi-cycle snapshot evidence rather than BUILD-scale validation), and a different failure mode (content selection by the orchestrator), and nesting it here would hide that it is the thinner of the two. The authority sub-decision, by contrast, has no meaning outside this architecture and is nested. Two ADRs, not one or three. Presented to the practitioner at the DECIDE gate as a default they may override.

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- The corpus gains a human-facing read at the resolution the moment calls for, generated rather than maintained.
- Audit-substance and provenance are preserved in a form audits can check directly.
- The agent's context delivery can follow Gupta et al.'s recency-window finding instead of the flat corpus.
- Cycle 019's five ADRs, and any future cluster applying one pattern, have a form (one Pattern record plus entries) that is both compact and auditable.
- The six constraints turn the spike's failure modes into checkable requirements, each with its rejected alternative.

**Negative:**
- A new maintenance commitment: records must be authored and the derivation tool maintained. Constraint 6 names it; BUILD measures it, including the one-time cost of roughly 98 legacy index records under option C.
- Note Discipline and per-type schemas add authoring friction at exactly the moment (DECIDE) the practitioner already finds heavy. The bet is that authoring one record is less than writing one 400-line ADR that nobody reads.
- Scaling is unvalidated. The Conditional Acceptance status and Tier-2 anchoring are the honest markers.
- Under option C the corpus carries two kinds of ADR file (canonical legacy prose; derived views of records) and a reader must check the header. Surface Views mitigate this by being the intended read.
- The architecture inherits the kitchen-sink caveat and is single-cycle evidence at the topology level.

**Neutral:**
- The three-tier artifact hierarchy (ORIENTATION; product-discovery, system-design, roadmap; supporting material) may gain a fourth layer or absorb the Substrate into supporting material. ARCHITECT decides.
- The derived markdown ADR keeps the existing path convention so that links, hooks, and habits continue to resolve.
