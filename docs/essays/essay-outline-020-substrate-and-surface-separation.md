# Substrate-and-Surface Separation: A Methodology-Wide Architectural Move
*2026-05-22*

## Abstract

Cycle 019 closed on an open thread: did its five ADRs (094–098) represent five distinct calibration moves, or one underlying move expressed at unnecessary granularity? The user's "uneven topology" hypothesis at the DECIDE gate framed this as a granularity-vs-clarity tension extending across the corpus: ADRs accumulate at a granularity that serves agent context but obscures the user's ability to grasp the methodology's big picture, particularly at gate checkpoints and at graduation. Cycle 020 investigates the hypothesis and finds it empirically confirmed at the within-cycle level (5 ADRs apply one underlying Calibration-with-Graduation-Trigger Pattern at 5 workflow points); methodology-wide in mechanism (Sweller's element interactivity operates at every holistic-synthesis moment, not graduation alone); and structurally addressable through substrate-and-surface separation as a methodology-wide architectural commitment. A focused spike on the Cycle 019 5-ADR sample demonstrates that the architecture preserves audit-substance through derivation while surfacing six design constraints that any committed amendment must honor.

**CONCLUSIONS:**

- **C1.** The topology hypothesis is empirically confirmed at the within-cycle level. The five Cycle 019 ADRs apply one underlying Calibration-with-Graduation-Trigger Pattern at five workflow points; compression to one Pattern ADR plus five structured substrate entries preserves audit-substance, provided the substrate is treated as a first-class auditable layer.
- **C2.** The corpus-overwhelm pattern is methodology-wide, not graduation-scoped. Sweller's element interactivity is a well-supported structural mechanism for cognitive load at synthesis moments; the Loop 3 lit-review did not compare CLT against alternative frameworks (Hick's law; Miller's working-memory limits; information-density-vs-processing-speed), so the cycle's commitment is to CLT as a sufficient explanation, not as the only candidate. The mechanism operates at every holistic-synthesis moment (gate-checkpoints, architectural reviews, onboarding, graduation). Graduation has the widest simultaneous corpus scope but the mechanism is the same. The user's "no one reads them post-graduation" claim generalizes per Lethbridge, Singer, and Forward (2003) and adjacent literature.
- **C3.** The methodology should adopt **substrate-and-surface separation** as a methodology-wide architectural commitment, **with complementary gate-conversation-tooling improvements** (auto-resolve identifier references inline at gate prompts; inline excerpts of referenced ADRs / invariants / scenarios) shipping alongside the architectural commitment. Substrate: structured records with typed links carrying audit-substance and provenance. Surface: computed views derived single-source from substrate, with overview-first navigation, focus+context preserved at drill-in, and incompleteness signaled visibly. Per the post-gate grounding analysis (research log §Grounding loops 4 + 4b), gate-conversation-tooling addresses the gate-friction specifically; substrate-and-surface addresses multi-resolution viewing, graduation consolidation, and agent-corpus-consumption optimization. The two are complementary, not competing.
- **C4.** Six design constraints surfaced by empirical demonstration must be honored by any committed amendment: extensible schema (not fixed types); per-type record schemas (not universal); resolution of compression-loss at sub-argument level; explicit authority decision (substrate canonical vs. ADRs canonical); note-discipline required on every non-trivial link; substrate-maintenance overhead accounted for in methodology toolchain.
- **C5.** The methodology's prior audience-bifurcation lineage (ADRs 064, 083, 084, 085, 092, 095) is convergent but incomplete under one reading; under the alternative reading (strengthened by post-gate Loop 4 cross-cycle topology evidence — see Section 9 META) the prior ADRs were complete answers to genuinely different problems and Cycle 020 is identifying a new architectural problem rather than completing a trajectory. Both readings remain held open at DECIDE; the cross-cycle evidence supports the alternative reading more than the cycle's initial framing acknowledged.
- **C6.** The architecture is committed; the specific schema language is deferred to DECIDE and ARCHITECT. The lit-review supports a class of typed-link plain-text formats; the spike showed that any pre-committed schema under-covers the corpus. The methodology amendment commits the architecture (substrate-and-surface separation, single-source derivation, focus+context, note discipline) without pre-committing to a specific format.
- **C7.** Held caveat: the Calibration-with-Graduation-Trigger Pattern itself inherits the kitchen-sink risk (ADR-097 Target 2 discriminating-criterion limit) — and **by extension the substrate-and-surface architecture inherits the same risk**, since the architecture is what the pattern's application produces. Both the pattern and the architecture's diagnostic value depend on whether they produce actionable distinctions at artifact-creation moments, not whether they can be retrospectively applied. The post-gate Loop 4 cross-cycle topology evidence (Section 9 META) strengthens this caveat: the 5-ADR-cluster-applying-one-pattern that Cycle 019 produced is anomalous, not characteristic of other cycles' ADR sets. The pattern as methodology-level vocabulary recurs (083 in Cycle 017; 091 in Cycle 018; 094–098 in Cycle 019 all apply variants), but the topology-level clustering does not. Multi-cycle observation per the cycle's graduation-trigger pattern (ADR-097, ADR-098 lineage) is the appropriate response.

  - EVIDENCE: (Cycle 020 research log §Grounding Loop 4b) Direct analysis on the user's specific friction example showed gate-prompt-rendering with auto-resolved identifier references addresses the gate-friction directly but leaves three other concerns unaddressed (multi-resolution viewing per Shneiderman 1996; graduation consolidation per Lethbridge 2003 + the user's lived practice; agent corpus consumption per Gupta 2026).
  - SYNTHESIS (within this section): the methodology amendment is joint — substrate-and-surface architecture plus gate-conversation-tooling improvements as complementary commitments.
- SYNTHESIS: Substrate-and-surface separation is empirically grounded; the architecture is supported by recent retrieval-accuracy studies, classical information-visualization literature, and the cycle's own spike demonstration. The methodology amendment should commit the architecture; specific format and tooling are downstream concerns. The post-gate grounding analysis adds that gate-conversation-tooling ships as a complementary commitment (see Section 9 META and ADR-CANDIDATE-020.5).

### Section 5: Design constraints surfaced by empirical demonstration (C4)

- CLAIM: Six design constraints from the spike must be honored by any committed amendment. *(SCOPE QUALIFICATION: the constraints are surfaced from a single spike on a single corpus sample (Cycle 019's 5 ADRs); the spike was tractable at this scale but multi-corpus scaling friction is not tested.)*
- CLAIM: Extensible schema, not fixed types.
  - WARRANT: Pre-committed schemas under-cover the corpus; the relationship vocabulary the corpus actually needs is more diverse than design-time enumeration anticipates.
  - EVIDENCE: (Cycle 020 research log §Loop 5.3 Constraint 1) Starting four typed-link types (`supersedes`, `implements`, `motivates`, `applies-pattern`) covered roughly half the relationships the five Cycle 019 ADRs needed. Six new types surfaced during authoring.
- CLAIM: Per-type record schemas, not universal record shape.
  - WARRANT: Universal record shape forces every record-type into the same field set; this either produces empty/thin fields or interpretive stretching, both replicating the arc42 / C4 comprehensiveness-trap failure mode.
  - EVIDENCE: (Cycle 020 research log §Loop 5.3 Constraint 2) Spike's five-field audit-substance block fit ADR records but stretched uncomfortably for the Pattern record. (Cycle 020 lit-review §Q5) arc42 and C4 comprehensiveness traps are documented analog failure modes.
- CLAIM: Compression-loss at sub-argument level must be resolved.
  - WARRANT: Source ADRs carry first-class sub-decision structure that flat schemas cannot hold without nested fields; ignoring this produces silent lossy compression.
  - EVIDENCE: (Cycle 020 research log §Loop 5.3 Constraint 3) ADR-094's signal-evaluation step (mechanism specification — no field for it in flat schema). ADR-097's Target 2 and Target 3 sub-decisions (each with its own commitment-and-rationale shape). ADR-098's reflexive-application argument (structurally distinct from empirical warrants but flattened into them).
- CLAIM: Authority question must be resolved explicitly (substrate canonical vs. ADRs canonical).
  - WARRANT: The choice between (a) substrate canonical with derived ADRs and (b) ADRs canonical with derived substrate is consequential for authoring practice and maintenance discipline; defaulting it produces hidden assumptions in the implementation.
  - EVIDENCE: (Cycle 020 research log §Loop 5.3 Constraint 4) Spike implicitly assumed (b) — source ADRs canonical, substrate derived through interpretation. The choice was not deliberated; the consequence is that substrate updates require translation, not mechanical extraction.
- CLAIM: Note discipline required on every non-trivial link.
  - WARRANT: Typed links carry category information but not semantic payload; cross-reference resolution depends on adjacent note text.
  - EVIDENCE: (Cycle 020 research log §Loop 5.3 Constraint 5) Spike's `references → Invariant-0/4/8` links without note text remained cold-lookup-required despite typed-link affordances. Discipline of mandatory notes converts links from "category labels" to "co-located semantic content."
- CLAIM: Substrate-maintenance overhead must be accounted for in methodology toolchain.
  - WARRANT: Substrate authoring is interpretive work, not mechanical transformation; at corpus scale, the maintenance cost is substantial and must be a methodology-recognized toolchain artifact.
  - EVIDENCE: (Cycle 020 research log §Loop 5.3 Constraint 6) Spike authoring at five ADRs was tractable; scaling to ~100 ADRs (full RDD corpus) introduces cross-cycle link consistency, provenance link rot, namespace collisions, and cycle-archive interaction concerns that the spike did not test.
- SYNTHESIS: The architecture is supported; the constraints are real and binding. The methodology amendment commits the architecture (C3) while recognizing the six constraints as design-time concerns DECIDE and ARCHITECT must address before BUILD.
