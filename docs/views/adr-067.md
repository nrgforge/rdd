## Summary

Adopts the Three-Tier Enforcement Classification as the methodology-level taxonomy operationalizing Invariant 8's anchoring requirement: Skill-Structure Layer (step-anchored dispatch in skill text, ADR-065), Harness Layer (PostToolUse dispatch log plus Stop hook manifest compound check, ADR-063/064), and User-Tooling Layer (graduating conversational mechanisms to artifact-producing form, ADR-066). A mechanism proposal is classified by a four-step decision procedure; one that fits none of the three substrates cannot be specified as unconditional (Tier 1) and must be marked best-effort or contingent instead. The three substrates are not a priority hierarchy — each is primary in its own domain — and a single mechanism may legitimately be anchored to more than one substrate at once.
