## Summary

Establishes the Inversion Principle at two levels: a procedural step within /rdd-discover producing the Assumption Inversions section (ADR-007 §4), and a cross-cutting principle applied as a habit of mind across RESEARCH, PRODUCT DISCOVERY, DECIDE, and ARCHITECT, with ARCHITECT flagged as particularly important because architecture is where product assumptions calcify most permanently. The Inversion Principle is the mechanism serving the product dimension of the strengthened Invariant 0, not a new invariant of its own, preserving the coherence of the existing invariant set (0-7).
