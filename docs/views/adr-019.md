## Summary

Adds ORIENTATION.md as a new top-tier artifact in a three-tier Artifact Hierarchy: Tier 1 Orientation (ORIENTATION.md, entry point that routes readers), Tier 2 Primary readable (product-discovery.md, system-design.md, read end-to-end by their respective audiences), and Tier 3 Supporting material (domain-model.md, essays, ADRs, scenarios, consulted for provenance and depth), amending the prior two-tier design principle. The document addresses a gap confirmed by stakeholder feedback: no artifact previously answered "what is this system, who is it for, and where do I go next?" for teammates receiving handoffs, AI agents bootstrapping sessions, or reviewers needing rapid orientation.
