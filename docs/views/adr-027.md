## Summary

Enriches the SYNTHESIS framing conversation (ADR-013 Phase 3) with four simultaneous dimensions to navigate: discovery type (which novelty signal is strongest and what structural affinity it carries), narrative form (established patterns, hermit-crab forms, and meta-forms), audience constraint (medium as creative catalyst, not delivery spec), and epistemic posture (determined, exploratory, or indeterminate stance toward certainty). The existing narrative lenses (ABT, story spine, volta placement) and the three inversions (ADR-017) remain available as tools, organized within these dimensions rather than replaced.
