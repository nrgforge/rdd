## Summary

Fixes the Orientation Document's structure at exactly five sections, in order: (1) What this system is — one paragraph of essential purpose, not features or architecture; (2) Who it serves — stakeholder summary compressed from product discovery; (3) Key constraints — the top 3-5 quality attributes or invariants from the domain model that shape every decision; (4) How the artifacts fit together — the Artifact Hierarchy with one-line descriptions and when to read each; and (5) Current state — completed phases, settled decisions, and remaining Open Questions. No section should exceed a few short paragraphs, and the entire document must be readable in under five minutes.
