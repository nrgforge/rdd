## Summary

The `argument-auditor` specialist subagent's responsibilities are extended to include Argument-Graph consumption, pyramid graph-traversal, and expansion-fidelity verification when the primary document is an Essay-Outline (per ADR-092), anchored in `agents/argument-auditor.md` as Skill-Structure Layer instructions rather than domain-model vocabulary alone. Essay-Outline becomes a fourth named genre; the auditor traverses the four-tier pyramid (Abstract, Argument-Graph, Citation-Embedded Outline, References), verifies the Pyramid Refinement compliance test at three boundaries plus reverse-direction, assigns P1/P2/P3 severity to violations, and reports a pyramid coverage map and expansion-fidelity findings. The three existing genres' behavior is unchanged.
