## Summary

Adds a Research Methods Subagent, the eighth specialist subagent, that reads the research question set before the research phase begins and applies belief-mapping to each question, flagging questions that embed conclusions, presuppose architectures, or narrow the hypothesis space prematurely. The narrow first version produces a research design review artifact at `./.rdd/audits/research-design-review-NNN.md`; cross-cycle methodological pattern detection across research logs is deferred to a second version. It is dispatched by the orchestrator before /rdd-research's first research loop, and again before any research loop following a substantial essay revision, as a Tier 1 unconditional mechanism.
