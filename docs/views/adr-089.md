**Type:** ADR (index) · **Status:** Proposed · **Cycle:** 017 · **Canonical source:** `docs/decisions/adr-089-adr-067-harness-layer-revision.md`

**Workflow point:** Three-Tier Enforcement Classification taxonomy — Harness Layer row and decision-procedure step 2 · **Mechanism:** amended Harness-Layer technique description (advisory disposition) and State-C detectability claim; substrate primacy unchanged

## Summary

Partially supersedes ADR-067: the Three-Tier Enforcement Classification's Harness Layer row is amended to describe its two hooks (PostToolUse dispatch log; Stop-hook compound check) as operating in advisory disposition rather than blocking, and the decision-procedure's step 2 and the "only structural defense" State-C claim are revised from impossibility to detectability, reflecting ADR-088's advisory demotion of ADR-064. Substrate primacy is explicitly preserved: the Harness Layer remains primary for phase-boundary and silent-dispatch-fallback mechanisms; only its enforcement strength (not its domain coverage) changes.
