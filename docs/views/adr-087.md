## Summary

The methodology adopts validation spikes as an optional step in research phases, holding the Beck-port framing (RDD as a port of Beck's time-value-of-money tidying argument) explicitly as an unverified claim rather than a load-bearing premise. Tightly-scoped prototyping is adopted as a research method alongside lit-review, literature search, and methods review, triggered (felt, not mechanical) at the research → discover gate when a claim risks downstream over-elaboration or when interaction-grounding would surface questions lit-review alone cannot reach; the practitioner records rationale whether or not a spike is run. The cycle-as-instance reflection — that research-phase findings are research-not-validation until tested against the world — is encoded as a standing scope-of-claim caveat on the corpus.
