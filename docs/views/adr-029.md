## Summary

Establishes that SYNTHESIS is usually terminal but can re-enter RESEARCH when structural experimentation (ADR-028) surfaces an incoherence the pipeline has not addressed, the writer cannot explain the material to the identified audience, or the framing conversation surfaces an unaddressed question. Re-entry is the writer's decision, not the agent's recommendation, and must be scoped narrowly to a specific research question at a specific scope, with the finding recorded in the research log before the pipeline loops back.
