## Summary

The `**In-progress phase:**` field's role is advisory-noise suppression, not phase-manifest-check enforcement; the field is optional and its absence does not produce a correctness failure. Introduced in v0.8.2 as a load-bearing predicate preventing a Stop-hook blocking loop, the field is obsolete in that role after ADR-088's v0.8.3 advisory demotion (the manifest check can no longer block, so it cannot cascade); its remaining function is to suppress the repeated missing-artifacts advisory during expected mid-phase work. Set at phase entry and removed at phase-exit readiness by the orchestrator, per the existing per-entry header field lifecycle.
