## Summary

Establishes that the synthesis phase does not add a separate epistemic gate after its outline deliverable; instead the three-phase conversation (ADR-013) itself subsumes the gate function — Journey Review requires retrieval and articulation, Novelty Surfacing requires reflection and self-explanation, and the Framing Conversation requires articulation and generation. The synthesis essay, written outside the pipeline, is the terminal epistemic act of the entire RDD cycle, satisfying Invariant 1 at its highest level, while Invariant 2 is satisfied because every conversational step is generative rather than mere approval. This resolves the domain model's open question of whether synthesis needs its own gate.
