## Summary

A Tier-2 felt-trigger at agent-facing artifact creation, parallel to ADR-083's human-facing Outcome Test: does this artifact produce direct agent consumption without re-orientation cost, or does it feel additional to the agent-facing corpus? Consumer-naming is necessary but not sufficient (trivially-passing answers fail the spirit). Graduation trigger names a candidate /rdd-conform audit step.
