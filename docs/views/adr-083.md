## Summary

Human-facing artifacts are admissible when they produce direct human understanding without AI as workaround (the Outcome Test); the default intervention on a reading failure is in-place restructuring of the existing artifact; a bolt-on or felt-additional artifact is the failure-mode signal. A methodology-wide default with named exception conditions.
