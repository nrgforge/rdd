## Summary

Establishes structural experiments — quick, externalized, diagnostic trials in a candidate form (a paragraph, a sketch, a few bullet points) — as the framing conversation's primary mechanism for navigating the four dimensions of ADR-027, replacing discursive discussion of forms with trying them on. The agent proposes experiments and may draft a starting version, but the writer must produce their own; a failed experiment is treated as diagnostic, clarifying what the material needs, rather than wasted effort.
