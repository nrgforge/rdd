## Summary

When ARCHITECT assigns a qualitative claim ("transparent," "seamless," "backward-compatible," etc.) to a module's responsibility, the system-design step requires the claim to be decomposed into one or more testable fitness properties, recorded as `**Fitness:** <observable property> — <how observed/measured>` adjacent to the claim, before the ARCHITECT gate may pass. The qualitative claim is retained as the human-readable summary; the fitness properties are the verification anchors. Fitness properties carry forward into BUILD, becoming Cycle Acceptance Criteria Table entries (ADR-073) or preservation scenarios (ADR-075) where applicable, addressing the ARCHITECT-phase share of the Issue #13 failure chain.
