## Summary

Lifecycle composition is named as a third category of integration risk in build stewardship, alongside test-suite composition (Composable Tests) and structural-boundary verification (Integration Verification + Stewardship Tier 1), covering risk at lifecycle boundaries where ownership and mutation rights over shared mutable state are ambiguous. The build skill is extended at three existing anchor points: a Lifecycle Composition subsection in Composable Tests (design-time prompt), a lifecycle-sequence verification paragraph in Step 5 Integration Verification (verification-time anchor), and a new sub-item 6e "Shared mutable state" under Stewardship Tier 1's Test quality item (review-time detector). The extension is scoped to the build skill only, anchored per Invariant 8 in the Skill-Structure Layer.
