## Summary

Genuine Structural Resistance operates in two tiers: Tier 1 unconditional architectural mechanisms (specialist subagent dispatch for audits, susceptibility snapshot at phase boundaries, research-methods review, mandatory structural output sections) that no context relaxes, and Tier 2 context-responsive conversational mechanisms calibrated by phase vulnerability, engagement quality, and content trajectory.
