# Substrate-and-Surface Separation

[← Overview](overview.md)

**Type:** Term (concept) · **Cycle:** 020

## Definition

Cycle 020's central architectural commitment (C3): audit-substance and provenance live in the Substrate; humans read Surface Views derived from it; single-source derivation (a toolchain artifact, not a discipline) prevents dual-maintenance drift. Complemented, not replaced, by gate-conversation tooling that inlines referenced content at the point of use (Grounding Loop 4b). The architecture is committed; the record format is deferred to DECIDE and ARCHITECT (C6). Inherits the kitchen-sink caveat: its diagnostic value depends on producing actionable distinctions at artifact-creation moments (C7).

**Product origin:** "user-friendly and also still enabling for agentic flows" (Solo Developer, research log §User reframing)
