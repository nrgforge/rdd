## Summary

FF1 (practitioner-originated framing absorption is not audited by any specialist subagent) and FF3 (in-place amendments to RESEARCH artifacts during downstream phases have no re-audit protocol) are recorded as recognized gaps at vocabulary level; structural amendment is deferred with two-direction graduation triggers (harm evidence or reliance evidence over 2–4 cycles). The R5 post-gate narrow re-audit pattern is named as the current compound defense.
