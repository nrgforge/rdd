## Summary

Replaces the fixed-template epistemic gate protocol across all skill files with an Attend-Interpret-Decide (AID) cycle: the agent reads the cycle's conversation for engagement signals (Attend), forms a hypothesis about the user's engagement level — deep, adequate, surface, confused, or disengaged (Interpret) — and selects a contingent pedagogical move: challenge, probe, teach, clarify, or re-anchor (Decide). The gate is iterative and contingent on the user's response, ending when shared understanding is established rather than after a fixed number of turns; the decision is co-dependent with Invariant 4 Amendment 13, which shifts the constraint from duration to waste ('epistemic cost must be productive, not merely brief').
