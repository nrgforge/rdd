## Summary

Adds PLAY as an optional phase between BUILD and SYNTHESIS: the practitioner inhabits a stakeholder from Product Discovery through a three-movement structure (Inhabit, Explore, Reflect) using a Super-Objective (from discover) and Objective (from Interaction Specifications, ADR-037), bounded by felt understanding (Invariant 0) rather than a timebox (Invariant 4). Discoveries are recorded as Field Notes, categorized by feedback destination (missing scenario to DECIDE, usability friction to DISCOVER, new question to RESEARCH, challenged assumption to DISCOVER, delight to SYNTHESIS, interaction gap to Interaction Specifications), and play itself is the epistemic act, requiring no separate gate.
