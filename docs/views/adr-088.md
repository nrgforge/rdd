# ADR-088: ADR-064 Amendment — Phase-Manifest Check Demoted to Advisory; Compound Check Retained

**Type:** ADR (index) · **Status:** Proposed · **Cycle:** 017 · **Canonical source:** `docs/decisions/adr-088-adr-064-advisory-demotion.md`

**Workflow point:** Stop hook phase-manifest check and compound check, across all cycle modes · **Mechanism:** manifest check and compound-check findings demoted from blocking to model-visible advisory; skill-text anchoring (ADR-065) becomes the load-bearing enforcement layer

## Summary

Partially supersedes ADR-064: the Stop hook's per-phase manifest check no longer blocks under any condition, emitting a model-visible advisory instead (repeated each Stop until the artifact appears or `**In-progress phase:**` is set); the compound check (PostToolUse dispatch log + Stop-hook cross-reference) is retained but its fabrication-signal finding is also surfaced as advisory rather than block. ADR-064's State-C claim is amended from "structurally impossible" to "structurally detectable." With both checks advisory, skill-text anchoring (ADR-065) becomes the load-bearing enforcement layer; the Harness Layer's contribution shifts to visibility and detection, not prevention.
