## Summary

ADRs are the historical record and current-state artifacts carry currency; an accepted ADR's body is immutable while its status and a dated supersession or update header are mutable; a four-step supersession workflow and a drift-resolution decision tree govern changes. Since ADR-099 the immutable body of a post-ADR-099 unit is its Substrate record and the markdown file is derived.
