## Summary

Each skill in the Composable Skill Family prompts for available time during or immediately after Context Gathering and adapts along a continuous spectrum with three guideposts — Deep (30+ minutes), Standard (10-30 minutes), Focused (under 10 minutes) — scaling work decomposition breadth, refactor depth, and stewardship frequency accordingly. User validation of orientation is never skipped regardless of budget, and the time budget is advisory only: the skill does not watch a clock and adapts mid-session if the developer wants to go deeper.
