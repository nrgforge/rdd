## Summary

Establishes that the synthesis essay serves a dual purpose: a publishable narrative for the world outside the project, and a narrative context rollup for the pipeline itself. When a synthesis essay exists, the orchestrator treats it as a primary context source when bootstrapping a new session, since structured artifacts (ADRs, domain model, system design) answer what was decided, what vocabulary applies, and what was designed, but not what was discovered and why it matters. The quality bar for both functions is the same: an essay good enough to publish is good enough to orient a new session, and vice versa.
