## Summary

Extends ADR-010's inversion-principle governance with a third level: a narrative framing technique available to the synthesis writer during the framing conversation, offering three inversions as optional lenses — "what if the obvious takeaway is wrong," "what if the process is more interesting than the product," and "what if the reader's assumed context is the story." These are offered, not prescribed; the writer may use all, some, or none. ADR-010 is not superseded — its original two levels are unchanged; this adds a third, extending the relationship hierarchy to Invariant 0 → Inversion Principle → Assumption Inversions (product) / Narrative Inversions (synthesis framing).
