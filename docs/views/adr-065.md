## Summary

Specifies the Skill-Structure Layer implementation of Invariant 8's anchoring requirement: for every Tier 1 mechanism whose dispatch can be anchored to a concrete workflow step, the governing skill must name the mechanism by its `subagent_type`, place the dispatch at a mechanically-unavoidable step, use a canonical prompt skeleton with a regex-parseable `Output path:` line, and place the dispatch at a structurally privileged position (top or bottom third, not the "lost in the middle" zone) of the skill file. The primary fix adds a Susceptibility Snapshot Dispatch subsection to each of the eight phase skills at the phase-end position, writing to `.rdd/audits/susceptibility-snapshot-{cycle}-{phase}.md`; existing dispatch sites (citation, argument, research-methods auditors) are audited against the canonical skeleton and position.
