## Summary

At ADR-drafting time the dyad asks whether the commitment is empirically grounded by one of four paths (PLAY evidence; stakeholder-articulated demand; spike validation; structural operationalization); when none applies the ADR takes Conditional Acceptance or the adr-deferred- filename pattern. Two nested sub-decisions: Target 2 (the discriminating criterion is acknowledged, not resolved) and Target 3 (standalone at commitment time, in two senses). Tier-2; graduation trigger after 2–4 cycles.
