## Summary

The refactor skill embeds awareness of the full AI Smell Taxonomy (novel, exacerbated, accelerated tiers) in the Three-Level Refactor's level 1 (Smells) diagnostic step rather than delegating detection to a separate tool. Level 1 covers two code-inspectable smell catalogs, classical (Fowler) and AI-exacerbated (six patterns with AI-specific mechanisms); separately, the skill applies AI hygiene prompts for the three novel AI patterns (Constraint Decay, Slopsquatting, Intent Debt) that have no code-level signature and cannot be detected through inspection. Integration with the codebase-audit skill's Structural Health and Dependency & Coupling lenses is an optional supplement the build skill's stewardship checkpoints may invoke; the refactor skill's own diagnostic step does not require an audit.
