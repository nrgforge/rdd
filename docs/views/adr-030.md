## Summary

Specifies the SYNTHESIS outline as a two-register artifact: an argumentative backbone (claims follow from evidence, framing does not overreach; verified by the argument audit) layered with a curatorial arrangement (selection, juxtaposition, scale shifts, the shimmer, personal voice; assessed by writer-and-agent judgment, with no formal verification mechanism). The backbone must hold even in lyric, hermit-crab, or indeterminate forms; the argument audit's scope is unchanged and continues to check only the logical register.
