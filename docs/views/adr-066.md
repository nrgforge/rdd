## Summary

Conversational mechanisms that cannot be step-anchored or hook-verified are made governable by requiring them to produce an artifact at a canonical path (the gate reflection note is the first instance); the manifest check enforces existence and shape, not epistemic substance (the reframe-derailed gate limitation).
