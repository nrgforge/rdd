# Pivot Point

[← Overview](overview.md)

**Type:** Term (concept) · **Cycle:** 020

## Definition

A finding or decision that changes the destination: what gets built, who it is built for, or why (Invariant 0's own list). The unit that reaches the practitioner after Pre-Triage. Route-level choices (how to get there) are presented as overridable defaults; engine-level detail (the process being orchestrated) stays with the agent, inspectably. The criterion is the agent's gloss on the practitioner's ruling and is itself a candidate for refinement at DECIDE.

**Product origin:** "Come to me with real pivot points"; "sometimes we learn something new that needs to change the destination" (Solo Developer, DISCOVER gate 2026-09-03)

**Related:** Adjudication Load, Pre-Triage, Belief-Mapping Question, Grounding Reframe
